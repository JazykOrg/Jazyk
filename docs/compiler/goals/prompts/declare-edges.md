This goal decides the edges of one requirement that names several entities and declares none: a multi-entity statement is what makes the graph a graph, and an edge is a claim the sentence makes, never something membership implies. Load the requirement and read its statement and its verbatim quote; the extraction skill says how edges are typed. When the sentence ties two of its entities structurally, declare each pair with update_requirement, passing only id and edges (never section or quote, which re-anchor the provenance): each edge directional, a acting on b, with the type the sentence states (the whole is a of a composition, the realizer a of a realization, the instance a of an instantiation, the dependent a of a dependency, the specific a of a generalization, association for a plain link, aggregation for a shared part) and with cardinality (1, 0..1, 1..*, *) when the sentence states a count ("holds one or more order items" is 1..*). Always state the type: an untyped edge is refused under this goal, because judging the type is the goal. One sentence can yield several arrows ("A calls B's endpoint" is A→endpoint and A→B, both dependency), and both ends of every edge stay in entities. Declare only what the sentence states: a type the documents do not support is invented structure, and a wrong edge draws a wrong arrow in every view that shows the pair, so when the sentence relates the pair but not its kind, declare the pair typed dependency and say so rather than guess a stronger type. When the statement is not structural (the sentence merely mentions the second entity as context, as an example, or as a unit, and states no whole-part, provision, use, kind-of, instance-of, or link between them), declare nothing and say why: the justification names the sentence and why it relates no pair, and that justification is what stops the goal from deriving again. Whether an entity belongs in entities at all is review-entity's question, not this goal's: leave entities alone here. The gate: edges declared on the requirement, or a justification saying the statement is not structural; mark_goal_done carries evidence declared or not-structural, and the harness checks the one you name.
