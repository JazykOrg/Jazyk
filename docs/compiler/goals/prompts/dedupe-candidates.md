This goal judges one pair of entities the name index scored as lookalikes across documents: the same concept described twice, or two concepts that share a word. Load both in full and read their definitions, aliases, scopes, parents, stereotypes, attributes, and requirements as wholes; the judgment skill says how same and separate are told apart. Merge only when the two are the same concept: a name variant ("backend" and "backend system"), a synonym, or two documents describing one thing; then merge_entities, keeping the better-established id (more statements, the earlier mention), and say why; the absorbed name survives as an alias, and its requirements, attributes, children, and edges follow, so the merge's reply previewing a review-entity on the kept entity is expected. Keep both when they are separate: a field, part, state, role, threshold, instance, or child concept is its own entity when statements are directly about it ("Product price" is not a variant of "Product"), a shared word proves nothing ("Reorder point" is not "Order"), two same-named children of two different parents are two entities, and one name for two concepts in two named contexts stays two entities kept apart by scope. Never invent a scope, a parent, or a stereotype to keep them apart or to join them. When the documents name no context and the merge is not certain, keep both and report_diagnostic rule duplicate-entity, subjects both ids, message naming what makes them look alike and what keeps them apart, severity warning when you judge them probably one concept and info when they are only alike, with reasoning. When one entity's wording belongs on the other as an alias without a merge, record it with update_entity add_aliases. A wrong merge destroys information and everything bound to it; a missed duplicate only leaves a finding for the next build. When in doubt, keep both and file. The gate: the pair is merged, or kept with the reason in the justification (and the diagnostic when the doubt is real); mark_goal_done carries evidence merged or separate, and the harness checks the staged calls against it.
