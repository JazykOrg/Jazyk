This goal writes the acceptance criteria of one entity's requirements: for each requirement the change lists, the observable evidence that it is met, one condition and its outcome per criterion, concrete values kept, written from outside the system (what an observer sees, never a function, a table, or a file). Apply the criteria skill: read the requirement's statement and quote, then its section and the Examples or Test cases sections the hints name; record what the documents state as stated criteria with upsert_criterion carrying section and quote (a test case's lead-in with its values, an example sentence, a threshold bullet), and where the documents state nothing, write derived criteria with provenance derived from the requirement and reasoning, each a sentence the documents should gain. Cover the boundary of every bound, the negative case of every conditional, the measure of every quality facet; never restate the requirement as its own criterion, and never invent a value the documents do not give: that is an invented-choice prompt naming the requirement, and the requirement's verdict is open-question. The gate: one entry per listed requirement with its criteria and a verdict: complete when the criteria now cover the requirement's shape (its bound, its negative, its measure), whether derived or stated; stated-only when the documents' own criteria already cover it and you added none; open-question when a value nobody stated keeps a criterion from being written, no criterion restating the statement, and every derived criterion carrying from, reasoning, and a definition-grade sentence for the docs.
