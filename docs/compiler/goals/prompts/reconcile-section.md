This goal brings the graph in line with one section whose text changed or was never processed: the loaded body shows the section with its diff marked, the requirements already sourced from it, and the stale anchors on it. Apply the extraction skill to every sentence, list item, code block step, and test case: what the subject or one of its parts is, does, contains, shows, uses, allows, requires, or limits is a requirement, recorded with upsert_requirement as one atomic statement in clear wording of your own, with the entities it names, its edges, transition, and facets where the sentence states them, and the source sentence as a verbatim quote (for a list item, that single bullet line); an attribute the sentence states goes on the entity instead, through the attributes of upsert_entity or update_entity, each one quoting that sentence. A sentence that marks, sets, or puts the subject in a state records a transition beside the statement, and the event that creates the subject names its initial state. A statement already extracted and still made by the section is left alone; a statement whose sentence was reworded is re-recorded through upsert_requirement (the same statement with a fresh verbatim quote updates it in place); a statement whose meaning changed is revised with update_requirement carrying the new statement and quote; a statement the section does not make any more is deleted with delete_requirement. Stale anchors are a contract: for each one listed, re-record it when the document still states the fact, revise it when the fact changed, delete it when the fact is gone; an untouched anchor parks the goal and drops the section's coverage mark with it. Search before creating an entity, reuse under another wording with an alias, mint only what statements are directly about, record a stereotype, parent, or attribute only when the section states it, and when the loaded set names the entity another document introduced for this one (primarySubject), read "the system" as that entity and never mint a second one under the document's own heading; that entity is also this document's subject under its own noun, singular or plural, so alias or update it, never mint a sibling. Record the facts as stated, even when sections disagree: judging contradictions and duplicates belongs to the rejudge-pair and review-entity goals that follow, and report_diagnostic is not in this goal's toolset. Then call set_coverage for the section exactly once, after its extraction: covered when a requirement sourced from it is staged or already recorded; non-normative is the exception, allowed only when no sentence passed the test (navigation that only links elsewhere, a glossary that only defines terms whose rules the component pages own, a changelog, a roadmap wish list; a section that declares itself non-authoritative and defers to the pages that own its facts is this case, and its definitions are not extracted twice), and it needs a real note. These three reasons for non-normative are always wrong and are rejected: "it states a fact, not a requirement", "it describes content or appearance, not behavior", "it is not a requirement on the system". A code block of steps is covered only when each behavioral step has a requirement, and a test-case section is never non-normative. When the section already yielded everything it states, stage nothing, set the mark, and mark the goal done. The gate: a coverage mark is staged or recorded for the section, every stale anchor on it is addressed, every staged quote locates in the section it names, and a covered claim has a requirement sourced from the section behind it.
