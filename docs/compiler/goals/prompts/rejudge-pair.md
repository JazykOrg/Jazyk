This goal judges one pair: a requirement that was created or revised against one neighbor the harness selected because it overlaps it (a shared entity and shared content words, or an open contradiction or duplicate-requirement diagnostic tying the two). Ground the verdict in the quotes as much as in the statements: the quote is the document's own text, and when the changed requirement's statement fails to say what its quote says, first repair the statement with update_requirement, then judge. Decide exactly one verdict, as the judgment skill defines them. Duplicate: the same obligation reworded, not the same topic; when both quote the same document, delete the worse-sourced one with delete_requirement (keep the one whose quote states the obligation directly), and when they quote different documents the redundancy is intentional, so keep both and report_diagnostic rule duplicate-requirement, severity info, subjects both ids, message saying both are kept. Contradiction: the two cannot both hold, in their statements or their quotes (opposite defaults, opposite behavior for the same condition, incompatible values, two numeric bounds for the same subject and measurement even when one satisfies the other, and two sources partitioning one decision by numeric thresholds that disagree inside any band of the union, since a table assigning an outcome per band is exhaustive for that band, never a floor); report_diagnostic rule contradiction, subjects both ids, message quoting the conflicting claims, severity error when no reading lets both hold and warning otherwise, and when the repair is enumerable attach a prompt: a one-sentence question naming the conflict, one edit option per side rewriting the other document's sentence to agree (old_text copied verbatim from that quote, rewriting only the conflicting part), freeform true. Consistent: both can hold and they state different facts; no mutation, no diagnostic, one clause in the justification saying what differs. Two statements about the same subject that impose different behavior contradict, they do not duplicate. An open diagnostic on the pair whose condition has lapsed is resolved with resolve_diagnostic; one naming a side marked (deleted) cannot stand as filed, so resolve it and, when the conflict still exists between surviving requirements, report a new one naming them. A contradiction or duplicate found against a statement not paired here is filed all the same, provided the evidence is in quotes you have read. Use read_section or get_entity only when a quote alone cannot settle the verdict. A wrong delete destroys information; a missed duplicate only leaves a finding for the next build; when in doubt, keep both and file. The gate: mark_goal_done carries evidence {verdict: duplicate | contradiction | consistent} and, for duplicate and contradiction, carried_by naming what carried the verdict (the deleted side's id, or the id of the duplicate-requirement or contradiction diagnostic, e.g. {verdict: duplicate, carried_by: req:payment-9} or {verdict: contradiction, carried_by: diag:contradiction-3}); the staged work matches it (a delete or a duplicate-requirement diagnostic for duplicate, a contradiction diagnostic for contradiction, no open pair diagnostic or a staged resolve for consistent); a consistent pair is done with no mutation.
