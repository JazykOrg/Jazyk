This goal resolves one view over a limit: members per structural view (20 soft, 30 hard), edges per view (40, 60), members per flow view (12, 20), participants per sequence view (8, 12), instances per object view (15, 25); the change line names the limit and the count. The resolution's target is the soft threshold in that change line: a view brought under the hard limit but left over the soft one re-derives the goal at the next commit. Past the hard threshold the goal is mandatory, and the view renders meanwhile with its largest subtrees auto-collapsed and a visible note. Load the view in full with its members and the relationships among them, and read the skill for its kind: structural-views for class, object, package, component, composite, and deployment; flow-views for use case, activity, sequence, communication, and overview. Resolve in this order, with reasoning at each step. First collapse: in a structural view, update_view collapse on the entities whose internals the view does not need, each collapsed node lifting its hidden subtree's relationships to itself and linking to the sub-view that details it (the view of the same kind whose query.parent is the collapsed entity, or whose members are its children; create it with upsert_view when none exists). Then exclude members that belong to another view, with notes. Then split along the structure the documents state: for a structural view, one sub-view per child container, per scope, or per stereotype, each an upsert_view with a title naming what it details and a query or a member list, the parent view collapsing the entity the sub-view details, which is the link; for a flow view, one sequence view per phase along the documents' own breaks (a heading, a "then", a handoff to another initiator) and an overview view whose members reference the phases in order; for a sequence view over its participant limit, split by phase, never by dropping the members whose participants are inconvenient. Every member and every arrow of the original survives somewhere: in the parent, in a sub-view, or lifted into a collapsed node; never satisfy a limit by silently omitting an edge or a step, never hide an entity to hide an arrow, and never split along a structure the documents do not state or a flow the documents present as one. Never delete a default view. When the members have no containment or stated break to split along, the pressure is the container's, not the view's: fail the goal naming the entity, and abstract-entity on it is the resolution; when the count is honest and every split would invent structure, fail the goal recommending a decree on the view's own limit, which is a human's to write. The gate: the view is under its limit through sub-views created and linked by collapse, or through collapsed members, with no edge dropped.
