# Characters

Four people carry the book. Ines and Theo carry the point of view; Callum and Dot are
seen only through them. Each character is introduced by age and occupation, then by what
they carry into the story.

## Ines Marlow

Ines Marlow. Age 31. Occupation: bookseller. She owns and runs the Lantern Room, the
bookshop on the quay, and has done since her mother died four years before the book
opens. She was born in Port Alder and has never lived anywhere else. Ines is a member of
the Marlow family, the last of it still living in the town.

Ines reads standing up. She keeps a pencil behind her ear and writes the day's takings
in the same ledger her grandmother used. She is quick to anger and slow to admit she was
wrong, and the book makes her do both.

Dot has called her Nessa since she was six, and Callum picked the name up at school.
Nobody else uses it, and Ines does not answer to it from strangers.

## Theo Brandt

Theo Brandt. Age 34. Occupation: marine surveyor. He works freelance for whoever is
paying, and this autumn Pryce Maritime is paying: Callum hires Theo to survey the harbor
wall before the demolition. Theo knows nobody in Port Alder when he arrives.

Theo keeps a field notebook and writes every measurement in it twice, once on site and
once again that evening. He does not lie, but he is comfortable not saying things. He
has moved eleven times in twelve years and owns nothing he cannot carry.

## Callum Pryce

Callum Pryce. Age 33. Occupation: project lead at Pryce Maritime, his father's company.
The marina is the first project with his name on it. Ines and Callum went to school
together and were close until they were nineteen.

Callum believes the marina will save the town, and the book lets him believe it. He is
not a villain and is not written as one, but he cuts the lease page out of the harbor
ledger, and he answers for it in chapter 7.

## Dot Feeney

Dot Feeney. Age 58. Occupation: ferry office manager. She runs the ferry office on the
quay and has done for thirty years. Dot has known Ines since Ines was six. Dot knows
everyone who steps off the ferry and everything that happens on the quay, and she keeps
the harbor ledger in the ferry office safe.

Dot is the first person in the book to know who Theo works for, and she tells Ines.

## The Marlow family

The Marlow family is Ines's family. The family has held the lease of the Lantern Room
for three generations, since Ines's grandmother signed it in 1961. The lease grants the
Marlows the quay frontage for as long as the harbor wall stands, which is why the lease
matters. Ines is the only Marlow who appears in the book; her grandmother and her mother
are spoken of but never seen.

## Who knows whom

- Ines and Callum know each other from school.
- Dot and Ines know each other; Dot is the nearest thing Ines has to family in the
  town.
- Callum and Theo know each other only through the contract: Callum hires Theo.
- Ines and Theo have never heard of each other before chapter 1.
- Dot and Theo meet when Theo steps off the ferry.
