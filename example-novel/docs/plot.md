# Plot

Eight chapters, in reading order. Each scene below is one line naming who acts and what
they do; the manuscript expands every line into prose and adds nothing that changes what
happens. Scenes inside a chapter run in the order listed. The chapter titles are the
file names of the [deliverable](./README.md#the-deliverable) without the number.

## Chapter 1: Arrival

- Theo arrives in Port Alder on the afternoon ferry with one bag and a case of survey
  instruments.
- Dot meets Theo at the ferry office and sends him along the quay to the Lantern Room
  for a room.
- Ines lets the upstairs room to Theo without asking what brings him to town.

## Chapter 2: The survey

- Theo walks the harbor wall at low tide and finds a crack in the footing directly
  beneath the Lantern Room.
- Callum tells Theo to leave the crack out of the written report, because a wall that
  can be repaired is a wall the council will not demolish.

## Chapter 3: The ledger

- Dot tells Nessa who Theo works for.
- Ines asks Dot for the harbor ledger, the book in the ferry office safe that records
  every lease on the quay.
- Ines finds the Marlow lease in the harbor ledger and reads the clause that grants the
  frontage for as long as the wall stands.
- Ines decides to take the lease to the harbor council and block the demolition.

## Chapter 4: The quay

- Ines confronts Theo on the quay about the survey and asks him what he found.
- Theo refuses to show Ines the report and tells her to ask Callum.

## Chapter 5: The council

- Callum asks Nessa to drop the petition, for old times' sake.
- Ines presents the harbor ledger to the harbor council to block the demolition.
- The plan fails: when Ines opens the ledger at the meeting, the lease page has been cut
  out, and the council votes to proceed with the demolition.
- Theo offers Ines the crack finding as proof that the wall can be repaired instead of
  replaced.

## Chapter 6: The storm

- Dot closes the ferry office when the storm warning comes and turns the last ferry
  back.
- Theo shores up the crack with the harbor crew through the night of the storm.
- Ines keeps the lantern lit and the kettle on for the crew until dawn.

## Chapter 7: The notebook

- Ines finds Theo's field notebook in the upstairs room and reads the measurements he
  wrote down twice.
- Ines gives the notebook to the harbor council as evidence that the crack was known
  and left out of the report.
- Callum admits to the council that he cut the lease page out of the ledger and
  withdraws the marina plan.

## Chapter 8: The Lantern Room

- Theo turns down the next contract and stays in Port Alder.
- Ines asks Theo to stay, and he tells her he already has.

## A worked scene

One scene written out the way the [deliverable](./README.md#the-deliverable) defines a
scene, to check that the definition holds. The notebook scene is the first scene of
chapter 7:

- Point of view: Ines.
- Setting: the upstairs room of the Lantern Room.
- Goal: Ines wants to put Theo's notebook back in his bag before he notices it is out.
- Outcome: she keeps it, reads it, and takes it to the council.

Every scene in the manuscript can be written out this way. This one is the check that
the template holds, and chapter 7 of the manuscript opens with it.
