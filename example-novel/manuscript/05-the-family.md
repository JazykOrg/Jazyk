# The family

The Marlow family was Ines Marlow's family, and that was the first thing to know about her. She was a member of the Marlow family, and she was the last of it still living in Port Alder, the last Marlow the town had left, and the town knew her for that.

Ines was the only Marlow who appeared in the book. Her grandmother and her mother were spoken of but never seen, the two women who had held the lease before her, and Ines was the one who stood in the room and lit the lantern and kept the kettle on, the only Marlow a reader would meet.

The lease was the thing that made the family matter. The lease granted the Marlows the quay frontage for as long as the harbor wall stood, and that was why the lease mattered: the frontage was the family's, the quay was the family's, and the family held it for as long as the wall held, and the wall was the clock that would tell them when the frontage was gone.
