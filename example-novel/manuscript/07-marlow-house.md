# Marlow House

Marlow House was the Marlow family's house on Chapel Lane in Port Alder, up the hill from the harbor. From the quay you could see it, the house on the lane, and from the house you could see the quay, and the two looked at each other across the town.

The house had been in the family as long as the shop lease, and the two were spoken of together, the house and the lease, the way a key and a lock are spoken of together. The lease had granted the frontage and the house had held the family, and the family had held both, and no one in the town spoke of the house without the lease or of the lease without the house.
