# Marlow House

Ines lived in Marlow House alone.

The house on Chapel Lane was hers, the last of the family's keeping, and she kept it by herself. There was no one else in it. The grandmother had died in the room below the house, and the mother had gone away from it, and Ines was the one who stood in it now, and she stood in it alone, the only resident the house had left, and the town knew her for that, the woman who lived up the hill in the old house by herself.

No scene of the book was set inside Marlow House. The house was seen from the quay, the house on the lane up the hill, and it was mentioned, the way a thing is mentioned that a reader is not meant to enter, and it was never entered. No one in the book went up the hill and into the house, and no one described its rooms, and no event of the book took place within it. It was the house on the lane, seen from the water and spoken of from the quay, and that was the whole of it.
