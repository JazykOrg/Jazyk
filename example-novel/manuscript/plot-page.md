# Plot page

The plot page is the planning record that tracks the plot of the book. It is kept in
sync with the outline: when a chapter comes in short it is cut from the outline, and
this page is changed to match the cut, so the plot and the manuscript stay consistent.
No chapter is padded to reach a length; a short chapter is cut, and this page is changed
to match.

## Structure

The book runs eight chapters. The odd chapters belong to Ines and the even chapters
belong to Theo. Chapter 1 opens in Ines's head watching the ferry come in, and chapter 8
closes in Theo's head.

## Plot beats

- The harbor wall is coming down. Pryce Maritime has bought the quay and plans to replace
  the wall and the row of shops on it with a marina.
- Theo Brandt is the marine surveyor Pryce Maritime hires to survey the wall before the
  demolition. A harbor wall survey has three parts: a walkover, core samples, and a
  written report.
- Theo walks the wall at low tide and finds a crack in the footing directly beneath the
  Lantern Room. He has already decided that Ines should know about the crack.
- Callum tells Theo to leave the crack out of the written report, because a wall that can
  be repaired is a wall the council will not demolish.
- Ines finds the Marlow lease in the harbor ledger and reads the clause that grants the
  frontage for as long as the harbor wall stands.
- Ines decides to take the lease to the harbor council and block the demolition.
- Ines confronts Theo on the quay about the survey and asks what he found.
- Ines presents the harbor ledger to the harbor council to block the demolition.
- The council votes to proceed with demolishing the harbor wall, because the lease page
  has been cut out of the ledger and the clause cannot be read.
- Theo offers Ines the crack finding as proof that the wall can be repaired instead of
  replaced.
- The storm comes in off the north. Theo shores up the crack with the harbor crew through
  the night, and the crack holds.
- When Theo shores up the wall through the storm, Ines's stance toward Theo changes from
  ally to beloved.
- Ines gives Theo's field notebook to the harbor council as evidence that the crack was
  known and left out of the report.
- Callum withdraws the marina plan.
- The wall stays up.

## Characters

- Ines Marlow is the only Marlow who appears in the book. She keeps the Lantern Room,
  lit the lantern, and keeps the kettle on.
- Theo Brandt is the marine surveyor.
- Callum Pryce is the project lead at Pryce Maritime.
- Dot Feeney runs the ferry office on the quay and keeps the harbor ledger safe.

## Setting

Port Alder climbs a hill above a stone harbor wall, and the quay runs along the top of the
wall. The ferry is the only way into Port Alder that does not require a two-hour drive
around the estuary. The harbor wall was built in 1840 and was last surveyed in 1988. The
harbor council owns the wall and leases the quay frontage to the shops standing on it.
