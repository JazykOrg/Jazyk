# Pryce Maritime

Pryce Maritime was the company that had bought the quay, and the buying was the thing that made the rest of it possible. It was a small company, the kind that a family ran, and the family was the Pryces, and the company was the thing the Pryces had run for a generation and a half, the way a family runs a thing that has been theirs longer than the family itself.

Callum Pryce was the project lead at Pryce Maritime, and Pryce Maritime was his father's company. He was thirty-three, and the company was the thing his father had built and the thing Callum now ran, and the marina was his first named project, the first thing he had put his name to in the company's books. Callum was the project lead, and the project lead was the man who ran the marina, and the marina was the thing that would stand where the wall had stood, and the company was the thing that had bought the quay and planned the marina, and Callum was the man who made the plan go.

Theo Brandt worked freelance, and a freelance man worked for whoever was paying. He had done a hundred harbor wall surveys, and every one of them had been a job he took from whoever had the money for it, the council or a company or a private owner, and the work was the thing he took and the payer was the thing that decided who he worked for. This autumn Pryce Maritime was paying. It was the autumn of the survey, and the survey was the thing the marina needed, and Pryce Maritime was the company that had bought the quay and planned the marina, and so Pryce Maritime was the payer, and Callum was the man who hired Theo to survey the harbor wall before the demolition, and the hiring was the thing that put Theo to work for the company that autumn.
