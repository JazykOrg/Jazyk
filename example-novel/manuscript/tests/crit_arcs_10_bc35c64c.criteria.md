# crit_arcs_10_bc35c64c

Criterion: Each stance-change trigger appears as a scene in the plot rather than as an off-page change.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must depict every stance-change trigger as an on-page plot scene. A reader evaluating the manuscript must be able to identify each trigger in the narrative itself rather than infer it from summary, outline notes, backstory, or an unexplained change in attitude.

1. Every stated stance change has a corresponding scene in the manuscript.
2. Each trigger occurs on the page through action, dialogue, narration, or interiority available to the reader.
3. No stance change happens only between scenes, in backstory, in synopsis-like summary, or without a depicted event.
4. The triggering scene must be part of the plot, not only a test note or metadata outside the manuscript.
