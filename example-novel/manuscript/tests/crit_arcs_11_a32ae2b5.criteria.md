# crit_arcs_11_a32ae2b5

Criterion: Before Ines confronts Theo at the chapter 5 council, Ines does not know that Theo has decided she should know about the crack.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must preserve Ines's ignorance of Theo's decision until the chapter 5 council confrontation.

1. The manuscript must contain a chapter 5 council confrontation involving Ines and Theo.
2. Before Ines confronts Theo at that council, Theo must already have decided that Ines should know about the crack.
3. Before that confrontation, Ines must not know that Theo has made this decision.
4. Ines must enter the confrontation believing Theo is her enemy, or otherwise acting from a mistaken hostile understanding consistent with not knowing his decision.
