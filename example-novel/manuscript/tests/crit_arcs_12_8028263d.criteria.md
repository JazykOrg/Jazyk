# crit_arcs_12_8028263d

Criterion: In chapter 5, Ines enters the council believing Theo is her enemy.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a chapter 5 council scene in which Ines enters while believing Theo is her enemy.

1. Chapter 5 must contain an identifiable council scene or council meeting.
2. Ines must enter, arrive at, or walk into that council scene.
3. At the moment she enters, the narration, dialogue, interiority, or immediately surrounding action must show that Ines believes Theo is her enemy or regards him with equivalent hostile opposition.
4. The belief must belong to Ines at entry into the council, not only before chapter 5 or after the council scene has begun.
