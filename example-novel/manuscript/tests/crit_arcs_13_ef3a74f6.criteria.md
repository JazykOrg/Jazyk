# crit_arcs_13_ef3a74f6

Criterion: No scene contains both an Ines stance change and a Theo stance change.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep Ines's stance changes and Theo's stance changes in separate scenes. A reader evaluating the manuscript must be able to identify every scene where either lead's stance changes and confirm that no single scene contains both leads changing stance.

1. Every Ines stance change is identifiable in a specific scene.
2. Every Theo stance change is identifiable in a specific scene.
3. For each scene, at most one of the two leads changes stance in that scene.
4. A scene that contains one lead's stance change may affect or reveal the other lead, but it must not also complete a stance change for the other lead.
