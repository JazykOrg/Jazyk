# crit_arcs_14_c9e06cbe

Criterion: Across the stance sequence stranger, acquaintance, rival, ally, beloved, neither Ines nor Theo changes to an earlier stance.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep both leads' relationship arcs moving forward through the stance sequence without regression. A reader evaluating the manuscript must be able to identify the ordered stance progression for Ines toward Theo and Theo toward Ines and confirm that neither lead later returns to an earlier stance after reaching a later one.

1. Ines's stance toward Theo must never move from beloved, ally, rival, or acquaintance back to any earlier stance in the sequence stranger, acquaintance, rival, ally, beloved.
2. Theo's stance toward Ines must never move from beloved, ally, rival, or acquaintance back to any earlier stance in the same sequence.
3. Temporary conflict, doubt, concealment, or tension may occur only if the text still preserves the lead's current or later stance rather than reestablishing an earlier stance.
4. This forward-only ordering must hold across the whole manuscript, not merely within isolated chapters or scenes.
