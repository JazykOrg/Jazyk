# crit_arcs_15_7664bfb4

Criterion: At the start of the story, both Ines and Theo have stranger as their stance.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish at the start of the story that Ines and Theo begin as strangers to each other.

1. The opening or earliest relevant scenes must show Ines and Theo before any meaningful relationship has formed.
2. Ines's stance toward Theo must be identifiable as stranger: she does not know him, recognize him as personally familiar, or already hold an acquaintance, rival, ally, or beloved stance.
3. Theo's stance toward Ines must be identifiable as stranger: he does not know her, recognize her as personally familiar, or already hold an acquaintance, rival, ally, or beloved stance.
4. Any later acquaintance or relationship development must occur after this initial stranger stance is established for both leads.
