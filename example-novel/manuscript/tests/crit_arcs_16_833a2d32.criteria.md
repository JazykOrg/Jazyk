# crit_arcs_16_833a2d32

Criterion: At the end of the book, both Ines and Theo are beloved and neither lead moves out of beloved afterward.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must leave both leads in the beloved stance at the end of the book. A reader evaluating the ending must be able to confirm that Ines regards Theo as beloved, Theo regards Ines as beloved, and no later scene, narration, dialogue, or epilogue moves either lead out of that stance.

1. By the ending, Ines's stance toward Theo must be identifiable as beloved through narration, dialogue, action, or interiority.
2. By the ending, Theo's stance toward Ines must be identifiable as beloved through narration, dialogue, action, or interiority.
3. The book must leave both leads in that beloved stance; no subsequent passage may move either lead back to stranger, acquaintance, rival, ally, or any other non-beloved stance.
4. The ending state must be shown in the manuscript itself, not only asserted in outline notes, tests, or external documentation.
