# crit_arcs_18_6b3a2831

Criterion: Before Theo's next contract gives her a reason to leave, Ines has had no reason to leave Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that, before Theo's next contract creates or offers a reason for Ines to leave Port Alder, Ines has previously had no reason to leave Port Alder:

1. The manuscript must contain a passage before or at the relevant turning point involving Theo's next contract.
2. Before that turning point, the text must show or clearly imply that Ines has had no prior reason to leave Port Alder.
3. The absence of a prior reason to leave must belong to Ines, not another character.
4. No earlier passage may contradict this by showing that Ines already had a reason to leave Port Alder.
