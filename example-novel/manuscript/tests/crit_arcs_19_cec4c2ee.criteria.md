# crit_arcs_19_cec4c2ee

Criterion: When Theo's next contract gives Ines a reason to leave Port Alder, Ines declines it.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a turning point in which Theo's next contract creates or offers Ines a concrete reason to leave Port Alder, and Ines chooses not to take that reason:

1. The manuscript must establish Theo's next contract as a new or upcoming contract connected to Theo.
2. That contract must give Ines a clear reason, opportunity, or invitation to leave Port Alder.
3. Ines must decline, refuse, reject, or otherwise choose not to leave because of that contract.
4. The refusal must belong to Ines and must answer the reason to leave created by Theo's next contract, not an unrelated offer or departure.
