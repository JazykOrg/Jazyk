## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show Ines's relationship with Theo progressing through exactly four distinct stance changes:

1. Stranger to acquaintance: Ines initially does not know Theo and they become acquainted.
2. Acquaintance to rival: Their relationship shifts from acquaintance to rivalry or opposition.
3. Rival to ally: They move from being rivals to working together or supporting each other.
4. Ally to beloved: Their alliance deepens into love or romantic attachment.

Each transition must be identifiable in the narrative through scenes, dialogue, or narration that marks the shift. The total number of stance changes must be exactly four, no more and no fewer.
