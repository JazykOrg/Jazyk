# crit_arcs_21_ee5c99fc

Criterion: A reader following the plot finds the midpoint scene is foreshadowed by the first half, looked back on by the second half, and lands both leads at once.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain an identifiable midpoint scene. A reader evaluating the full manuscript must find all of these conditions:

1. Events, concerns, images, conflicts, or stated goals in the first half point toward the midpoint scene.
2. Events, consequences, memories, reversals, or decisions in the second half look back on the midpoint scene as a meaningful turning point.
3. The midpoint scene changes or lands the arc of both leads in the same scene, not only one lead or two disconnected moments.

The scene may satisfy these conditions through plot action, dialogue, interior narration, or recurring motifs, but the relationship between both halves of the manuscript and both leads must be evident to a reader rather than only stated in outline notes.

## Verdict contract

PASS when the midpoint scene is foreshadowed by the first half, looked back on by the second half, and lands both leads at once, evident to a reader. FAIL when any of the three conditions is absent or only stated in outline notes.
