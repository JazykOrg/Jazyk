# crit_arcs_22_546afd8d

Criterion: A stance change appears as an event inside a scene and is not delivered as summary narration.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show each stance change as an on-page event inside an identifiable scene. A reader evaluating the manuscript must be able to locate the moment of changed stance in scene action, dialogue, interiority, or immediate scenic narration, not only in retrospective or synopsis-like summary.

1. Each stance change has an identifiable scene in the manuscript.
2. The change occurs during the scene through observable action, dialogue, interior response, or immediate scenic narration.
3. The manuscript does not deliver the stance change only through summary narration, retrospective explanation, outline-like transition, or an off-page statement that the relationship has changed.
4. The scene itself must let the reader infer or recognize the new stance without relying on external notes or metadata.
