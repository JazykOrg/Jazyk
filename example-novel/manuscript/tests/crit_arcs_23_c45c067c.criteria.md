# crit_arcs_23_c45c067c

Criterion: The reader can see each stance change before the lead admits that change to themselves.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must make every lead stance change visible to the reader before the lead explicitly recognizes, names, or admits that change internally or aloud.

1. Each stance change has an identifiable visible cue before the lead's self-admission.
2. The cue is available to the reader through scene action, dialogue, behavior, sensory detail, or immediate narration rather than external notes.
3. The lead's later admission occurs after that visible cue, not before or at the same moment without prior evidence.
4. This ordering holds for every stance change in both leads' arcs.
