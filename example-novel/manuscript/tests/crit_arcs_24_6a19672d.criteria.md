# crit_arcs_24_6a19672d

Criterion: At Theo's arrival in Port Alder, the text identifies Theo as `36` years old.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a passage depicting or referencing Theo's arrival in Port Alder in which his age is stated or clearly indicated as 36:

1. Theo arrives in Port Alder (or the arrival is referenced in narration, dialogue, or internal monologue).
2. At or near that moment, the text states that Theo is 36 years old, either directly (e.g. "thirty-six", "36") or through unambiguous context (e.g. a birthdate that makes him 36 at the time of arrival).
3. The age must be 36 specifically, not a different number.
