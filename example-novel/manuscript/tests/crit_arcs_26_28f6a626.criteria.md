## Pass conditions

The manuscript must demonstrate that by the conclusion of Theo's character arc, he has stopped his pattern of moving from job to job. This can be shown through:

1. Theo commits to staying in one place or one role, rather than drifting between freelance assignments.
2. The narrative explicitly marks or implies the end of his transient work pattern (e.g., deciding to remain in Port Alder, accepting a permanent position, or choosing to settle).
3. The shift is tied to his arc's progression (stranger to acquaintance), showing that his connection to the place or people is what stops him.

The text must contain identifiable scenes, dialogue, internal monologue, or narration that show Theo is no longer moving on to the next job.
