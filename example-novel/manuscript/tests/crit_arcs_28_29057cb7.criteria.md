# crit_arcs_28_29057cb7

Criterion: When Theo finds the crack beneath the Lantern Room, Theo's stance toward Ines changes from `stranger` to `acquaintance`.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which:

1. Theo discovers a crack beneath the Lantern Room (the bookshop).
2. This discovery occurs while Theo's stance toward Ines is still that of a stranger.
3. As a direct consequence of or in close narrative proximity to this discovery, Theo's stance toward Ines shifts to acquaintance.

The transition must be evident through narrative action, dialogue, or internal reflection that marks Theo beginning to know Ines personally rather than remaining a stranger.
