# crit_arcs_2_a1a86b61

Criterion: When Theo takes the upstairs room, Ines's stance toward Theo changes from stranger to acquaintance.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or narrative passage in which Theo takes the upstairs room (moves into it, agrees to stay there, or is shown settling in), and this event causes or coincides with Ines's stance toward Theo shifting from stranger to acquaintance.

1. Before the event, Ines and Theo are strangers: they have not met, or have had no meaningful interaction.
2. The event itself is depicted: Theo takes, accepts, or moves into the upstairs room.
3. After or during the event, Ines's stance toward Theo is identifiably that of an acquaintance: they have been introduced, exchanged words, or begun a relationship beyond strangers.
4. The transition is causally linked to Theo taking the upstairs room, not to an unrelated event.
