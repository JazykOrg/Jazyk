# crit_arcs_30_c6099dcc

Criterion: When Ines's plan fails at the council, Theo's stance toward Ines changes from `rival` to `ally`.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene at or around the council meeting in which Ines's plan visibly fails. In the same scene or its immediate aftermath, Theo's attitude toward Ines must shift from rivalry or opposition to support or alliance. The shift must be identifiable through narration, dialogue, or action that shows Theo siding with, defending, or cooperating with Ines after having previously opposed her. The trigger for the change must be the failure of Ines's plan, not an unrelated event.
