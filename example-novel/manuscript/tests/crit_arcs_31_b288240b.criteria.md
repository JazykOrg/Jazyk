# crit_arcs_31_b288240b

Criterion: When Ines asks Theo to stay, Theo's stance toward Ines changes from `ally` to `beloved`.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or moment in which Ines asks Theo to stay (in Port Alder, or with her, or not to leave). In response to or as a consequence of that request, Theo's internal stance toward Ines must shift from viewing her as an ally to recognizing her as beloved. This shift must be identifiable through narration, internal thought, dialogue, or behavior that marks the transition from alliance to love or deep romantic attachment.

Both elements must be present and linked:
1. Ines explicitly or clearly asks Theo to stay.
2. Theo's stance toward Ines changes from ally to beloved as a result of or in connection with that request.
