# crit_arcs_33_257eb645

Criterion: When Theo finds the crack under Ines's shop, the text presents Ines as real to Theo before she is presented as anything else to him.

## Pass conditions

In the manuscript, the scene where Theo discovers the crack beneath Ines's shop must present Ines to Theo as a real, concrete person (through physical detail, action, or sensory presence) before she is presented to him through any other framing (romantic interest, obstacle, ally, abstraction, or role).

1. The crack discovery scene must exist and involve Theo finding a crack under or beneath Ines's shop.
2. In that scene or its immediate aftermath, Theo's first impression or awareness of Ines must be grounded in her reality (her physicality, her presence, her concrete existence) rather than in any social, romantic, or functional role.
3. No prior scene may present Ines to Theo primarily through another lens (e.g., as a love interest, a rival, a merchant) before this grounding moment occurs.
