# crit_arcs_34_ee87c6fe

Criterion: On the Harbor Wall at low tide, Theo has already decided that Ines should know about the crack.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage set on the Harbor Wall at low tide in which Theo has already made the decision that Ines should know about the crack.

1. The scene or passage must be located on the Harbor Wall.
2. The timing must be low tide.
3. Theo must have already decided, before or by this moment, that Ines should know about the crack.
4. The decision must concern telling, showing, or otherwise making Ines aware of the crack, not merely Theo thinking about the crack or Ines separately.
