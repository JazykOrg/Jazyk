# crit_arcs_35_aefd00a7

Criterion: At the confrontation, Theo gets the opening he has been waiting for to tell Ines about the crack.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a confrontation scene in which Theo receives the opportunity he has been waiting for to tell Ines about the crack.

1. The confrontation scene must exist and involve Theo and Ines.
2. The scene must create a clear opening, opportunity, or natural moment for Theo to speak to Ines.
3. The opening must be the one Theo has been waiting for, not an unrelated chance.
4. Theo must use, or be positioned to use, that opening to tell Ines about the crack.
