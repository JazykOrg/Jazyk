# crit_arcs_3_a88df427

Criterion: When Dot tells Ines who Theo works for, Ines's stance toward Theo changes from acquaintance to rival.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which:

1. Dot reveals to Ines that Theo works for a particular employer (e.g. Pryce Maritime or whichever entity the narrative establishes as the catalyst).
2. As a direct consequence of learning this information, Ines's attitude or stance toward Theo shifts from acquaintance to rival.
3. The shift is identifiable in the narrative through dialogue, internal monologue, narration, or action that marks Ines now regarding Theo as a rival rather than a mere acquaintance.

The trigger for the transition must be Dot telling Ines who Theo works for, not some other event.
