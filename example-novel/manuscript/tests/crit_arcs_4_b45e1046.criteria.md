# crit_arcs_4_b45e1046

Criterion: When Theo offers Ines the crack finding, Ines's stance toward Theo changes from rival to ally.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Theo offers Ines the crack finding (a discovery, piece of evidence, or information about a crack). In response to this offer, Ines's stance toward Theo must visibly shift from rivalry or opposition to alliance or cooperation.

1. Theo explicitly offers or shares the crack finding with Ines.
2. Before the offer, Ines's attitude or behavior toward Theo is adversarial, competitive, or antagonistic (consistent with a rival stance).
3. After the offer, Ines's attitude or behavior toward Theo shifts to cooperative, supportive, or allied (consistent with an ally stance).
4. The transition is caused by the offer of the crack finding, not by an unrelated event.
