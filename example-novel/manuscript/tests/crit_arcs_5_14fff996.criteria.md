# crit_arcs_5_14fff996

Criterion: When Theo shores up the wall through the storm, Ines's stance toward Theo changes from ally to beloved.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Theo shores up the harbor wall during a storm. In response to or as a consequence of this act, Ines's stance toward Theo must visibly shift from alliance or cooperation to love or deep romantic attachment.

1. Theo actively shores up (reinforces, repairs, or holds) the harbor wall during a storm.
2. Before this event, Ines's attitude or behavior toward Theo is cooperative, supportive, or allied (consistent with an ally stance).
3. After this event, Ines's attitude or behavior toward Theo shifts to loving, devoted, or romantically attached (consistent with a beloved stance).
4. The transition is caused by Theo's act of shoring up the wall through the storm, not by an unrelated event.
