# crit_arcs_9_33476129

Criterion: When Ines confronts Theo about the survey, Theo's stance changes from acquaintance to ally.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Ines confronts Theo about the survey, meaning his survey work on the harbor wall for Pryce Maritime or the established survey work in the story. As a direct result of that confrontation, Theo's attitude or stance toward Ines must shift from acquaintance to ally.

To pass:

1. The confrontation scene must exist and involve Ines confronting Theo about the survey.
2. Before or at the start of the confrontation, Theo's stance toward Ines must be identifiable as acquaintance rather than stranger, rival, ally, or beloved.
3. During the confrontation or in its immediate aftermath, Theo must begin supporting, cooperating with, defending, or siding with Ines in a way that establishes an ally stance.
4. The shift from acquaintance to ally must be caused by the confrontation about the survey, not by an unrelated event.
