## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Dot Feeney's early knowledge of Theo Brandt's employer:

1. Dot Feeney is present or clearly identified in the manuscript.
2. Theo Brandt is present or clearly identified in the manuscript.
3. The text states or clearly indicates that Dot knows who Theo works for.
4. The text states or clearly indicates that Dot is the first person in the book to know who Theo works for.
5. The employer knowledge must concern Theo Brandt, not another character.
6. No earlier passage may show another character knowing who Theo works for before Dot does.
7. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes or character documentation.
