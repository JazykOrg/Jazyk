# crit_characters_1_7e0e5744

Criterion: A reader sees Callum hire Theo to survey the harbor wall before the demolition.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the hiring event:

1. Callum Pryce is present or clearly identified as the person arranging the work.
2. Theo Brandt is present or clearly identified as the person being hired.
3. Callum hires, commissions, contracts, or otherwise engages Theo for the work.
4. The work Theo is hired to do is a survey of the harbor wall.
5. The survey is positioned before the demolition of the harbor wall.
6. The evidence must appear in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, character documentation, or test criteria.
