Criterion: Ines has a pencil behind her ear and writes the day's takings in her grandmother's ledger.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines with both habitual details:

1. Ines keeps or has a pencil behind her ear.
2. Ines writes the day's takings in a ledger.
3. The ledger must be the same ledger her grandmother used, or otherwise clearly identified as her grandmother's ledger.
4. These details must belong to Ines and must be presented in the manuscript, not only in planning notes or character documentation.
