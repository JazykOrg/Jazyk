# crit_characters_22_c51be91d

Criterion: Ines gets angry quickly and later admits she was wrong.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show both sides of this character requirement:

1. Ines becomes angry quickly in a scene, exchange, or narrated moment.
2. Ines later admits that she was wrong.
3. The admission must be made by Ines, either aloud or in clear interiority.
4. The anger and later admission must both be present in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes or character documentation.
