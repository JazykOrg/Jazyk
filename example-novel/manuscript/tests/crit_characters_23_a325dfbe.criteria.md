# crit_characters_23_a325dfbe

Criterion: When a stranger calls Ines `Nessa`, Ines does not answer to the name.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish all of the following:

1. A stranger or otherwise unauthorized person calls Ines `Nessa`.
2. Ines does not answer, respond, or otherwise accept that address as her name from that stranger.
3. The scene or narration makes clear that `Nessa` is reserved for Dot and Callum, or that strangers are not permitted to use it.
4. These facts must be presented in the manuscript, not only in planning notes, character documentation, or this criteria file.
