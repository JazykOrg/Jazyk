# crit_characters_24_42ac98d6

Criterion: Ines and Theo carry point-of-view scenes, while Callum and Dot appear only through Ines's or Theo's point of view.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must use Ines and Theo as the only point-of-view carriers:

1. Ines must carry at least one point-of-view scene or passage through her narration, interiority, or close perspective.
2. Theo must carry at least one point-of-view scene or passage through his narration, interiority, or close perspective.
3. Callum must appear only as observed, interpreted, remembered, or discussed from Ines's or Theo's point of view.
4. Dot must appear only as observed, interpreted, remembered, or discussed from Ines's or Theo's point of view.
5. No scene or passage may enter Callum's or Dot's private interiority, narration, or close point of view.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to identify all point-of-view scenes and confirm that every such scene belongs to Ines or Theo, never Callum or Dot.
