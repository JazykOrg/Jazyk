Criterion: Until age 19, Ines Marlow and Callum Pryce are close schoolmates.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish all of the following:

1. Ines Marlow and Callum Pryce went to school together.
2. During their school years, Ines and Callum were close to each other.
3. Their closeness lasted until they were nineteen, or until age 19 is otherwise clearly identified as the endpoint.
4. These facts must be presented in the manuscript, not only in planning notes, character documentation, or this criteria file.
