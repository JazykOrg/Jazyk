# crit_characters_26_72fde84b

Criterion: Each character introduction gives the character's age and occupation before describing what the character carries into the story.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must introduce every carrying character in the required order:

1. Each carrying character has an identifiable introduction in the manuscript.
2. At each such introduction, the character's age is stated or clearly indicated before the text describes what the character carries into the story.
3. At each such introduction, the character's occupation is stated or clearly indicated before the text describes what the character carries into the story.
4. The carried-into-the-story material may be emotional history, motive, burden, habit, responsibility, or equivalent character freight, but it must come after the age and occupation.
5. No carrying character introduction may describe what the character carries into the story before giving both age and occupation.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to confirm this ordering from the manuscript itself rather than from planning notes or criteria files.
