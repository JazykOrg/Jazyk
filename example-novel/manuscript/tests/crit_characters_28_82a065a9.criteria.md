# crit_characters_28_82a065a9

Requirement: req:characters-42
Statement hash: 1c630d934951616e

Criterion: In the book, Ines Marlow appears and her mother and grandmother are mentioned but not seen.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that Ines Marlow is the only Marlow who appears in the book:

1. Ines Marlow appears directly in manuscript scenes or passages.
2. Ines's mother is spoken of, remembered, or otherwise mentioned, but does not appear directly in any scene.
3. Ines's grandmother is spoken of, remembered, or otherwise mentioned, but does not appear directly in any scene.
4. No other Marlow family member appears directly in the manuscript.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to distinguish appearing in the book from being mentioned or remembered.
