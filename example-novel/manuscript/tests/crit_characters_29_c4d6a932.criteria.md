# crit_characters_29_c4d6a932

Criterion: When Ines and Callum meet in the story, they recognize one another from school.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines and Callum's prior school connection through a meeting or encounter:

1. Ines Marlow and Callum Pryce meet, encounter one another, or share a scene in the manuscript.
2. During that meeting or its immediate context, both recognize one another or behave with clear mutual prior recognition.
3. The recognition is tied to their having known each other from school.
4. The prior school connection must be presented in the manuscript, not only in planning notes, character documentation, or this criteria file.
