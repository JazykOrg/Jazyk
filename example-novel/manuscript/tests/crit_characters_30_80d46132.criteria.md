## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that Ines Marlow and Theo Brandt have no knowledge of each other before chapter 1.

1. Before chapter 1 begins, the manuscript must not show or imply that Ines recognizes Theo's name, history, work, family, reputation, or prior presence.
2. Before chapter 1 begins, the manuscript must not show or imply that Theo recognizes Ines's name, history, work, family, reputation, or prior presence.
3. Chapter 1 or the earliest relevant scene must present their first awareness of each other as new, unknown, or otherwise not based on prior acquaintance or reputation.
4. No later passage may reveal a pre-chapter-1 connection, prior knowledge, or remembered familiarity between Ines and Theo.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the manuscript itself supports all pass conditions. Fail if the manuscript is absent, if either character knows of the other before chapter 1, or if the only support is planning text rather than manuscript content.
