# crit_characters_32_7783b468

Criterion: Ines owns and runs the Lantern Room on the quay four years after her mother died.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines Marlow's ownership and operation of the Lantern Room:

1. The manuscript names or clearly identifies Ines Marlow.
2. The text states or clearly indicates that Ines owns the Lantern Room.
3. The text states or clearly indicates that Ines runs the Lantern Room.
4. The Lantern Room must be identified as the bookshop on the quay.
5. The ownership and operation must be current four years after Ines's mother died, or otherwise clearly tied to her mother's death four years before the book opens.
6. No surrounding passage may contradict Ines owning or running the Lantern Room, its location on the quay, or the four-year timing after her mother's death.
