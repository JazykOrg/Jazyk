# crit_characters_34_d95444d7

Requirement: req:characters-8
Statement hash: caa4c35fc4296f77

Criterion: A reader encountering the Marlow family information sees Ines identified as a Marlow and as the last Marlow still living in Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines Marlow's place in the Marlow family and in Port Alder:

1. Ines Marlow must be named or otherwise clearly identified in the manuscript.
2. The Marlow family must be named or otherwise clearly identified in the manuscript.
3. The text must state or clearly imply that Ines is a member of the Marlow family.
4. The text must state or clearly imply that Ines is the last Marlow family member still living in Port Alder.
5. No surrounding passage may contradict Ines being a Marlow or being the last Marlow still living in Port Alder.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to identify both the family membership and the "last Marlow in Port Alder" status from manuscript content rather than from external notes alone.
