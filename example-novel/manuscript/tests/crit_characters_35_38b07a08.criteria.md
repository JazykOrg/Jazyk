Requirement: req:characters-9
Statement hash: bea0e83da2df18ad
Statement: Ines reads standing up.

Criterion: A reader seeing Ines read sees her standing while she reads.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines reading while standing:

1. The manuscript names or clearly identifies Ines Marlow.
2. A scene or passage shows Ines reading, not only owning books or working near books.
3. During that same reading action, Ines is standing or remains on her feet.
4. The standing posture must belong to Ines while she reads, not to another character or to a separate moment.
5. No surrounding passage may contradict Ines standing while she reads.
