# crit_characters_36_634efe18

Requirement: req:characters-43
Statement hash: 6faf44369396ea8f

Criterion: A reader of the book hears Ines Marlow's grandmother and mother mentioned but never sees either woman appear in a scene.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines Marlow's grandmother and mother as off-page family members:

1. Ines Marlow's grandmother is mentioned, remembered, discussed, or otherwise spoken of in the manuscript.
2. Ines Marlow's mother is mentioned, remembered, discussed, or otherwise spoken of in the manuscript.
3. Ines Marlow's grandmother does not appear directly in any scene as an acting, speaking, or present character.
4. Ines Marlow's mother does not appear directly in any scene as an acting, speaking, or present character.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to distinguish direct scene appearances from references, memories, or dialogue about these women.
