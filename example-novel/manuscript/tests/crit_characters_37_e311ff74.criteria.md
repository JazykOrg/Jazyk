# crit_characters_37_e311ff74

Requirement: req:characters-41
Statement hash: 10477338a998d9a7

Criterion: While the harbor wall stands, the lease grants the Marlows the quay frontage.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the lease's conditional grant of quay frontage:

1. The manuscript identifies the lease.
2. The manuscript identifies the Marlow family or the Marlows.
3. The manuscript identifies the quay frontage.
4. The text states or clearly implies that the lease grants the quay frontage to the Marlows.
5. The grant lasts while, or for as long as, the harbor wall stands.
6. No surrounding passage contradicts the grant, the beneficiaries, the quay frontage, or the harbor-wall condition.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to understand from manuscript content why the lease matters for the Marlows' quay frontage.
