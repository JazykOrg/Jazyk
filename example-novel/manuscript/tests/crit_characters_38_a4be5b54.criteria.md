# crit_characters_38_a4be5b54

Criterion: A reader identifies four carrying characters in the book: Ines, Theo, Callum, and Dot.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that exactly four people carry the book:

1. Ines is one of the carrying characters.
2. Theo is one of the carrying characters.
3. Callum is one of the carrying characters.
4. Dot is one of the carrying characters.
5. No additional person is presented as carrying the book at the same level as these four.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to identify these four characters as the book's carrying people from scenes, narration, viewpoint weight, or other manuscript content rather than from external notes alone.
