# crit_characters_39_41133d22

Criterion: A reader encountering the marina in Callum Pryce's story finds it identified as the first project with Callum Pryce's name on it.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the marina as Callum Pryce's first named project:

1. Callum Pryce is present or clearly identified in the manuscript.
2. The marina or marina plan is present or clearly identified in Callum's story.
3. The text states or clearly indicates that the marina is the first project with Callum Pryce's name on it.
4. The "first project" distinction must belong to Callum Pryce, not only to Pryce Maritime or another character.
5. No surrounding passage may contradict the marina being Callum Pryce's first named project.
