Criterion: A reader tracing Ines Marlow's family connection finds the Marlow family identified as Ines Marlow's family.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the Marlow family as Ines Marlow's family:

1. The Marlow family must be named or otherwise clearly identified in the manuscript.
2. Ines Marlow must be named or otherwise clearly identified in the manuscript.
3. The text must state or clearly imply that Ines belongs to, descends from, or is otherwise a member of the Marlow family.
4. No surrounding passage may contradict the Marlow family being Ines Marlow's family.

A reader evaluating the completed manuscript must be able to identify this family connection from manuscript content rather than from external notes alone.
