Criterion: In autumn, Theo works freelance and Pryce Maritime is the payer for his work.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Theo's autumn work arrangement:

1. Theo Brandt is present in the manuscript.
2. The text states or clearly indicates that Theo works freelance, or otherwise takes paid work from whoever is hiring him.
3. The text states or clearly indicates that the relevant timeframe is autumn.
4. The text states or clearly indicates that Pryce Maritime is paying Theo for the autumn work.
5. The payer relationship must belong to Theo's work, not to another character or unrelated transaction.
6. The evidence must appear in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes or character documentation.
