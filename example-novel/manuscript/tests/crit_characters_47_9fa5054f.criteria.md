# crit_characters_47_9fa5054f

Criterion: In a scene where Theo withholds information, Theo says no false statement while leaving something unsaid.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Theo's truthful-but-withholding behavior:

1. The manuscript contains a scene in which Theo withholds relevant information from another character.
2. In that scene, Theo leaves something material unsaid rather than openly disclosing it.
3. Theo makes no false statement in the scene; any spoken or narrated claim from him must remain true.
4. The withheld information and the absence of lying must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes or character documentation.
