# crit_characters_4_b8806513

Criterion: A reader sees Callum act on the belief that the marina will save Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show Callum's belief about the marina through narrative action:

1. Callum Pryce is present or clearly identified in a scene involving the marina or marina plan.
2. The text shows Callum believing, arguing, deciding, or acting as though the marina will save Port Alder or the town.
3. The belief must belong to Callum, not only to Pryce Maritime or another character.
4. The narrative must allow Callum to hold this belief sincerely rather than presenting it only as a known lie or villainous pretext.
