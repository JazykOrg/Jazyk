# crit_characters_8_ea062111

Criterion: Before Callum hires Theo, a reader has not seen Callum and Theo know each other outside the contract.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep Callum and Theo's prior connection limited to the contract:

1. The manuscript contains or clearly references the moment Callum hires Theo.
2. Before that hiring moment, the manuscript does not show Callum and Theo as friends, relatives, neighbors, social acquaintances, or otherwise personally connected.
3. Any knowledge between Callum and Theo before or at the hiring moment is limited to the contract, hiring, survey work, or business arrangement.
4. No earlier passage contradicts the limitation by showing them knowing each other outside the contract.
