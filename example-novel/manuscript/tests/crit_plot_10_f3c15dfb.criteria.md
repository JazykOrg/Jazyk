# crit_plot_10_f3c15dfb

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 5 finds Callum asking Nessa to drop the petition for old times' sake.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Callum asking Nessa to drop the petition in Chapter 5:

1. Chapter 5 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 5 contains an identifiable scene involving Callum and Nessa/Ines.
3. In that scene, Callum asks, requests, or urges Nessa/Ines to drop the petition.
4. Callum's request is made for old times' sake, or uses equivalent wording that clearly appeals to their past relationship.
5. The petition is the petition relevant to the Chapter 5 council conflict.
6. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
