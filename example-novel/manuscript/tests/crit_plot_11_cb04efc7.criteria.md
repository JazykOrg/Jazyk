# crit_plot_11_cb04efc7

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 5 finds Ines presenting the harbor ledger to the harbor council to block the harbor wall demolition.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines presenting the harbor ledger to the harbor council in Chapter 5:

1. Chapter 5 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 5 contains an identifiable harbor council scene or meeting.
3. In that scene, Ines presents, produces, shows, or submits the harbor ledger to the harbor council.
4. The presentation of the harbor ledger is intended to block, stop, prevent, or defeat demolition of the harbor wall.
5. The action belongs to Ines and is evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 5 is absent, the council scene is absent, Ines does not present the harbor ledger to the council, or the presentation is not tied to blocking the harbor wall demolition.
