# crit_plot_12_8a4367ef

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 5 finds that when Ines opens the harbor ledger at the meeting, the lease page has been cut out.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the missing lease page discovery in Chapter 5:

1. Chapter 5 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 5 contains an identifiable meeting or council scene involving Ines and the harbor ledger.
3. In that scene, Ines opens the harbor ledger.
4. When Ines opens the harbor ledger, the lease page is absent because it has been cut out.
5. The missing page must be the lease page relevant to the council conflict, not an unrelated page or document.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 5 is absent, the meeting scene is absent, Ines does not open the harbor ledger there, or the text does not show the lease page has been cut out at that moment.
