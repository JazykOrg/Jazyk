# crit_plot_13_7a4efed0

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 5 finds the council voting to proceed with the harbor wall demolition after the lease page is missing.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the council vote after the missing lease page discovery in Chapter 5:

1. Chapter 5 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 5 contains an identifiable council scene or meeting involving the harbor wall demolition.
3. In that scene, Ines opens or presents the harbor ledger and the lease page is missing or cut out.
4. After the missing lease page is discovered, the council votes, decides, or formally agrees to proceed with demolishing the harbor wall.
5. The vote or decision must belong to the council and must be a consequence of the failed ledger/lease-page evidence, not an unrelated decision elsewhere.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 5 is absent, the council scene is absent, the missing lease page is not discovered there, or the council does not vote or decide afterward to proceed with the harbor wall demolition.
