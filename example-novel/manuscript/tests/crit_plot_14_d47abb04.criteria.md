# crit_plot_14_d47abb04

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 5 finds Theo offering Ines the crack finding as proof that the harbor wall can be repaired instead of replaced.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Theo offering the crack finding to Ines as repair evidence in Chapter 5:

1. Chapter 5 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 5 contains a scene involving Theo and Ines.
3. In that scene, Theo offers, shares, presents, or explains the crack finding to Ines.
4. Theo frames the crack finding as proof or evidence that the harbor wall can be repaired.
5. The repair alternative must be contrasted with replacing the harbor wall or otherwise make clear that repair is possible instead of replacement.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 5 is absent, the Theo/Ines scene is absent, Theo does not offer the crack finding to Ines, or the crack finding is not used as proof that the harbor wall can be repaired instead of replaced.
