# crit_plot_16_28154bc6

Criterion: In Chapter 6, when the storm warning comes, Dot closes the ferry office.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Dot closing the ferry office in response to the storm warning in Chapter 6:

1. Chapter 6 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 6 contains or clearly references a storm warning.
3. Dot Feeney is present or clearly identified in the Chapter 6 storm-warning sequence.
4. Dot closes the ferry office when the storm warning comes, or the narration clearly states that she has closed it because of the warning.
5. The closure must be tied to the storm warning, not to an unrelated event.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 6 is absent, the storm warning is absent, Dot does not close the ferry office, or the closure is not connected to the storm warning.
