# crit_plot_17_469f0f83

Criterion: In Chapter 6, when the storm warning comes, Dot turns the last ferry back.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Dot turning the last ferry back in response to the storm warning in Chapter 6:

1. Chapter 6 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 6 contains or clearly references a storm warning.
3. Dot Feeney is present or clearly identified in the Chapter 6 storm-warning sequence.
4. Dot turns the last ferry back, refuses its departure, recalls it, cancels its crossing, or otherwise prevents that final ferry from continuing because of the warning.
5. The ferry action must be tied to the storm warning, not to an unrelated event.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 6 is absent, the storm warning is absent, Dot does not turn back the last ferry, or the ferry action is not connected to the storm warning.
