# crit_plot_18_6849436c

Criterion: In Chapter 1, Dot meets Theo at the ferry office and sends him along the quay to the Lantern Room for a room.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish this plot point in Chapter 1:

1. Chapter 1 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 1 contains a scene in which Dot and Theo meet.
3. The meeting takes place at the ferry office.
4. Dot sends Theo along the quay.
5. Dot directs Theo to the Lantern Room.
6. The purpose of sending Theo to the Lantern Room is to get a room (lodging).
7. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
