# crit_plot_1_b8d29ce1

Criterion: When Callum discusses the repairable crack with Theo, a reader sees him tell Theo to omit the crack from the written report.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show Callum pressuring Theo about the written report:

1. Callum and Theo are present or clearly identified in a scene discussing the harbor-wall crack.
2. The crack is repairable, or the scene clearly frames the wall as repairable because of the crack.
3. Callum tells, asks, instructs, pressures, or otherwise directs Theo to leave the crack out of the written report.
4. The scene connects the omission to the consequence that a repairable wall is a wall the council will not demolish.
5. The instruction must come from Callum and be directed at Theo.
