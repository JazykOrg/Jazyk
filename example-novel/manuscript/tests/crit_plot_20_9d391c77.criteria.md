# crit_plot_20_9d391c77

Requirement: req:plot-25
Statement: Ines Marlow keeps the lantern lit and the kettle on for the harbor crew until dawn.

Criterion: During the storm night, the harbor crew has the Lantern Room's lantern lit and the kettle on until dawn.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines Marlow keeping the Lantern Room ready for the harbor crew during the storm night:

1. Chapter 6 or an equivalent storm-night sequence exists in the manuscript.
2. Ines Marlow is present or clearly identified in that sequence.
3. The Lantern Room's lantern is kept lit for the harbor crew.
4. The Lantern Room's kettle is kept on for the harbor crew.
5. Both the lantern and kettle remain available until dawn, or the text clearly states that Ines keeps them so until dawn.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the storm-night sequence is absent, Ines does not keep both the lantern lit and the kettle on, the harbor crew is not the beneficiary, or the duration until dawn is not established.
