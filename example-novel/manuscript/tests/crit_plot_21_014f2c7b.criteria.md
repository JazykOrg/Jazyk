Criterion: A reader of the upstairs-room scene sees Ines find Theo Brandt's field notebook in the upstairs room.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain an upstairs-room scene in which Ines Marlow finds Theo Brandt's field notebook:

1. The scene occurs in the upstairs room or clearly identifies that location.
2. Ines Marlow is present in the scene.
3. The object found is Theo Brandt's field notebook, not a different notebook or document.
4. Ines finds the field notebook during the scene.
5. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the upstairs-room scene is absent, Ines is not the finder, the notebook is not Theo Brandt's field notebook, the location is not the upstairs room, or the fact appears only outside manuscript content.
