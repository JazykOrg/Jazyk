## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain an upstairs-room or field-notebook scene in which Ines Marlow reads Theo Brandt's duplicated measurements:

1. Ines Marlow has found or is handling Theo Brandt's field notebook.
2. The field notebook contains measurements written by Theo Brandt.
3. The measurements are shown or clearly described as having been written down twice.
4. Ines reads those twice-written measurements herself, rather than merely hearing about them from another character or summary.
5. The reading occurs after Ines finds the field notebook.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the field-notebook scene is absent, Ines does not read the measurements herself, the measurements are not Theo Brandt's, or the text does not show that the measurements were written down twice.
