# crit_plot_25_ff756aff

Criterion: When Theo rents the upstairs room, Ines lets it to him without asking why he has come to Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the following in its narrative:

1. Theo arrives at the Lantern Room (or the room above the shop) seeking lodging.
2. Ines, as the owner or operator of the Lantern Room, lets the upstairs room to Theo.
3. In the course of this transaction, Ines does not ask Theo what brings him to town, why he has come to Port Alder, or what his purpose is.
4. The absence of such a question must be evident in the scene: Ines's dialogue and narration show no inquiry into Theo's reason for being in Port Alder.
5. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

- PASS: The manuscript contains a scene in which Ines lets the upstairs room to Theo and, in that scene, she does not ask what brings him to town or why he has come to Port Alder.
- FAIL: The manuscript is absent, or Ines asks Theo what brings him to town / why he has come to Port Alder during the room-letting scene, or the scene does not exist.
