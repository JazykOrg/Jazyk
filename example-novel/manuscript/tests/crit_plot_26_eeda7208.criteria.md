# crit_plot_26_eeda7208

Criterion: A reader encounters eight manuscript chapters in reading order.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present eight chapters as the reading-order body of the novel:

1. The deliverable includes exactly eight manuscript chapter files or chapter sections intended for the reader.
2. The chapters are arranged in a clear reading order.
3. The order is complete and unambiguous, with no missing, duplicated, or extra manuscript chapters.
4. Test and criteria files do not count as manuscript chapters.
