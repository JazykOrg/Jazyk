# crit_plot_29_4787baff

Requirement: req:plot-37
Statement: The notebook scene's point of view is Ines.

Criterion: A reader of the notebook scene sees Ines as the point-of-view character.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a notebook scene whose point of view is Ines:

1. The notebook scene exists as identifiable manuscript content.
2. The scene involves Theo Brandt's field notebook or is otherwise the same notebook scene named by the requirement.
3. Ines Marlow is the scene's point-of-view character, shown through the narration's access to her perception, interiority, judgments, or immediate experience.
4. The scene does not shift into another character's point of view in a way that makes someone other than Ines the point-of-view character.
5. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the notebook scene is absent, Ines is absent, another character is the point-of-view character, or the point of view appears only outside manuscript content.
