## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain the notebook scene with the complete sequence of Ines's handling of Theo Brandt's notebook:

1. The notebook scene exists as identifiable manuscript content.
2. Ines Marlow keeps or retains Theo Brandt's notebook instead of returning it or leaving it behind.
3. Ines reads Theo Brandt's notebook herself.
4. Ines takes, brings, or carries Theo Brandt's notebook to the council.
5. The keeping, reading, and taking to the council all belong to Ines and occur in the notebook scene or its direct continuation.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the notebook scene is absent, Ines does not keep Theo Brandt's notebook, Ines does not read it herself, Ines does not take it to the council, or any part of the sequence appears only outside manuscript content.
