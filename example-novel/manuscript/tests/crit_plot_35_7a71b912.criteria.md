# crit_plot_35_7a71b912

Criterion: Every scene line in the plot names an actor and the action that actor takes.

## Pass conditions

The plot must make each scene line attributable and actionable:

1. Every scene line identifies the actor or actors who act in that line.
2. Every scene line states the action those actors take.
3. No scene line is only mood, theme, setting, or consequence without an acting subject.
4. No scene line depends on another line to supply the actor or the action.
