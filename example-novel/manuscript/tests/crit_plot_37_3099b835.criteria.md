# crit_plot_37_3099b835

Requirement: req:plot-42
Statement: Every scene in the manuscript can be written out with a point of view, setting, goal, and outcome like the notebook scene.

Criterion: The worked notebook scene shows the four fields point of view, setting, goal, and outcome, and any manuscript scene can be described with those same four fields.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must make the four-field scene model usable for every scene:

1. The worked notebook scene exists as manuscript scene content, not only as planning notes, tests, or criteria.
2. The worked notebook scene includes filled point of view, setting, goal, and outcome fields.
3. Every other identifiable manuscript scene can be described with those same four fields.
4. Each scene's point of view, setting, goal, and outcome are concrete enough for a reader to identify from the manuscript content.
5. No manuscript scene is missing one of the four required fields or depends on placeholder text for one of them.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript contains the worked notebook scene with the four filled fields and every manuscript scene can be checked against that same four-field model. Fail if the manuscript content is absent, the worked notebook scene is absent, any field is missing from the worked scene, or any manuscript scene cannot be described with point of view, setting, goal, and outcome.
