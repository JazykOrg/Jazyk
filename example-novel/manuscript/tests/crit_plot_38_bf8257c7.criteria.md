Criterion: Through the night of the storm, Theo and the harbor crew shore up the harbor wall crack.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Theo Brandt shoring up the harbor wall crack with the harbor crew through the night of the storm:

1. Chapter 6 or an equivalent storm sequence exists in the manuscript.
2. The sequence occurs during the storm and continues through the night, or clearly covers the night of the storm.
3. Theo Brandt is present and actively helps shore up, reinforce, brace, repair, or hold the harbor wall crack.
4. The harbor crew is present and works with Theo on the shoring effort.
5. The work concerns the harbor wall crack, not an unrelated repair or general storm response.
6. These facts must be evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the storm-night sequence is absent, Theo does not work with the harbor crew, or the work is not specifically shoring up the harbor wall crack.
