# crit_plot_39_c1fa80c2

Criterion: When the next contract is available, Theo turns it down.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Theo Brandt refuses the next available contract:

1. Theo Brandt is present or clearly identified in the relevant passage.
2. A next, new, or upcoming contract is available to Theo, offered to him, or otherwise established as his expected next work.
3. Theo turns down, refuses, declines, rejects, or chooses not to take that contract.
4. The refusal belongs to Theo and concerns the next contract, not a different offer or an unrelated decision.
5. The choice is evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the next contract is absent, the refusal is absent, the refusal belongs to someone other than Theo, or the decision concerns something other than the next contract.
