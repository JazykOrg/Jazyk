# crit_plot_3_20fab841

Criterion: After Callum admits cutting the lease page, a reader sees him withdraw the marina plan.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show Callum withdrawing the marina plan after his admission:

1. Callum admits that he cut the lease page.
2. After that admission, Callum withdraws, cancels, abandons, or formally pulls back the marina plan.
3. The withdrawal must be connected to Callum or his authority over the plan.
4. The event must concern the marina plan, not a different proposal.
5. The sequence must be clear enough that the withdrawal follows the admission.
