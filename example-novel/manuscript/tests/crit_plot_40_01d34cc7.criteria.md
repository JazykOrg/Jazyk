# crit_plot_40_01d34cc7

Criterion: After turning down the next contract, Theo remains in Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Theo Brandt stays in Port Alder after refusing the next available contract:

1. Theo Brandt is present or clearly identified in the relevant passage.
2. Theo has turned down, refused, declined, rejected, or chosen not to take the next available contract.
3. After that refusal, Theo remains in Port Alder, chooses to stay there, or is shown still living or staying there.
4. The staying belongs to Theo and occurs after the contract refusal, not before it or in an unrelated context.
5. The choice to stay is evident in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Theo's refusal of the next contract is absent, Theo's remaining in Port Alder is absent, the sequence is unclear, or the fact appears only outside manuscript content.
