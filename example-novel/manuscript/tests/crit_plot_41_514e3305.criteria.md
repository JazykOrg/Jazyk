Statement: Theo Brandt tells Ines Marlow that he has already decided to stay.

Criterion: When Ines asks Theo to stay, Theo tells Ines he has already decided to stay.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must contain a scene or passage in which Ines Marlow asks Theo Brandt to stay and Theo answers that he has already decided to stay:

1. Ines Marlow must explicitly or clearly ask Theo Brandt to stay.
2. Theo Brandt must hear or otherwise receive Ines's request before answering.
3. Theo must tell Ines that he has already decided to stay, or use equivalent wording that makes the decision prior to her request clear.
4. Theo's answer must be directed to Ines and must respond to her request to stay, not to an unrelated question or offer.
5. The request, response, and prior decision must appear in manuscript content, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Ines does not ask Theo to stay, Theo does not answer her, Theo's answer does not establish that he had already decided to stay, or the fact appears only outside manuscript content.
