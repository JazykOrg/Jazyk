# crit_plot_4_53b4a803

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 3 finds a scene in which Dot tells Nessa that Theo works for Pryce Maritime.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Dot telling Nessa who Theo works for in Chapter 3:

1. Chapter 3 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 3 contains a scene involving Dot and Nessa.
3. In that scene, Dot tells Nessa that Theo works for Pryce Maritime, or clearly identifies Pryce Maritime as Theo's employer or payer.
4. The disclosure must come from Dot and be directed to Nessa, not merely appear in narration or be told by another character.
5. The employer information must concern Theo Brandt, not another character.
6. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
