# crit_plot_5_a5c2755f

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 3 finds Ines asking Dot for the harbor ledger in the ferry office safe that records every lease on the quay.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines asking Dot for the harbor ledger in Chapter 3:

1. Chapter 3 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 3 contains a scene involving Ines and Dot.
3. In that scene, Ines asks Dot for the harbor ledger.
4. The harbor ledger is identified as being kept in the ferry office safe.
5. The harbor ledger is identified as recording every lease on the quay.
6. The request must come from Ines and be directed to Dot, not merely appear in narration or be made by another character.
7. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
