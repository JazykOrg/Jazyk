# crit_plot_6_537913bd

Requirement: req:plot-13
Statement hash: f4025bb96124d04b

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 3 finds Ines reading the Marlow lease clause that grants the quay frontage for as long as the harbor wall stands.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines finding and reading the lease clause in Chapter 3:

1. Chapter 3 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 3 contains a scene involving Ines and the harbor ledger.
3. In that scene, Ines finds or locates the Marlow lease in the harbor ledger.
4. Ines reads the lease clause herself, rather than only hearing about it from another character or summary.
5. The clause grants the frontage, quay frontage, or equivalent waterfront frontage to the Marlows.
6. The grant lasts for as long as the harbor wall stands, or equivalent wording with the same condition.
7. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if Chapter 3 is absent, the harbor-ledger lease scene is absent, Ines does not read the clause, or the clause's frontage grant and harbor-wall condition are not both present in manuscript content.
