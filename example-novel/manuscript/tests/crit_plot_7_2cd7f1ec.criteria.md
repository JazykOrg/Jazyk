# crit_plot_7_2cd7f1ec

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 3 finds Ines deciding to take the lease to the harbor council to block the harbor wall demolition.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Ines deciding to use the lease at the harbor council in Chapter 3:

1. Chapter 3 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 3 contains a scene involving Ines and the lease.
3. In that scene, Ines decides to take the lease to the harbor council.
4. The purpose of taking the lease to the council is to block, stop, or prevent the harbor wall demolition.
5. The decision must belong to Ines, not another character making the choice for her.
6. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
