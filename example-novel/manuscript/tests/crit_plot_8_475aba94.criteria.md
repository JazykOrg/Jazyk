# crit_plot_8_475aba94

Criterion: A reader of Chapter 4 finds Ines confronting Theo on the quay about the harbor wall survey and asking what he found.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the Chapter 4 quay confrontation:

1. Chapter 4 exists in the manuscript and is identifiable as the fourth chapter.
2. Chapter 4 contains a scene set on the quay.
3. Ines and Theo are both present in that quay scene.
4. Ines confronts Theo about the harbor wall survey or the established survey work.
5. Ines asks Theo what he found, or asks an equivalent question about the result of his survey.
6. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
