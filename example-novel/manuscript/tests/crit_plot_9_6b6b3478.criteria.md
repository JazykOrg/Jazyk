Criterion: A reader of Chapter 4 finds Theo refusing to show Ines the report and telling her to ask Callum.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Theo refusing Ines access to the report in Chapter 4:

1. Chapter 4 exists in the manuscript.
2. Chapter 4 contains a scene involving Theo and Ines.
3. In that scene, Ines asks for, demands, or otherwise seeks to see the report.
4. Theo refuses to show Ines the report, with the refusal coming from Theo and directed to Ines.
5. Theo tells Ines to ask Callum for the report, permission, or access to it.
6. The report in question is the written report connected to Theo's harbor wall survey.
7. These facts must be evident in the manuscript itself, not only in planning notes, criteria files, or character documentation.
