Criterion: A reader sees the novel begin with Theo's arrival on the ferry and end after the night of the storm and the week after it, all within one autumn.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present a single continuous autumn as its whole span, bounded by Theo's arrival and the aftermath of the storm.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. The opening of the novel presents Theo's arrival on the ferry as the beginning of the story.
3. The narrative reaches the night of the storm and continues through the week after it, so the ending falls after that week.
4. The entire span from Theo's arrival to the week after the storm is contained within one autumn; no season outside that single autumn is shown as part of the story's run.
