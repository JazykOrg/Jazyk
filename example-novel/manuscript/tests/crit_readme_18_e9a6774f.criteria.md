Criterion: A reviewer comparing the manuscript with the bible finds the manuscript honors the bible's characters, settings, chapter events, lead character changes, and prose style.

## Pass conditions

A reviewer who reads the manuscript alongside the bible must find that the manuscript honors the bible across all five dimensions:

1. Characters: the manuscript's characters match the bible's characters (who they are, their roles, their relationships).
2. Settings: the manuscript's settings match the bible's settings (where the story happens).
3. Chapter events: the manuscript's chapters carry the events the bible assigns to each chapter.
4. Lead character changes: the manuscript shows the two leads changing as the bible describes their changes.
5. Prose style: the manuscript's prose is written in the style the bible specifies.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. A side-by-side comparison of the manuscript and the bible shows agreement on characters, settings, chapter events, lead character changes, and prose style, with no contradiction in any of the five dimensions.
