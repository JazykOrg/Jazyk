# crit_readme_19_e1c66b6f

Criterion: By the week after the storm, the harbor wall is still up and no marina has been built.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep the marina unbuilt through the week after the storm:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. The harbor wall is still standing (up) by the week after the storm.
3. No marina has been built in place of the wall and row of shops by that point.
4. No passage shows the marina completed, opened, or in use by the week after the storm.

The requirement is a constraint: the marina Pryce Maritime plans to build must never appear as built. A passage that depicts the marina as constructed, opened, or occupied by the week after the storm violates the criterion.
