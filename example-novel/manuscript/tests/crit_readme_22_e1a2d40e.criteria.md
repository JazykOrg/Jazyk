Criterion: A reader of the premise sees Pryce Maritime own the quay and plan to replace the wall and row of shops with a marina that remains unbuilt.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript's premise must present Pryce Maritime as the owner of the quay and as the party planning to replace the harbor wall and the row of shops standing on it with a marina, and the marina must remain unbuilt.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. The premise (or an early establishing passage) identifies Pryce Maritime as having bought the quay.
3. That passage presents Pryce Maritime's plan to replace the wall and the row of shops standing on the quay with a marina.
4. The marina is not built; the plan does not proceed to completion within the narrative.
