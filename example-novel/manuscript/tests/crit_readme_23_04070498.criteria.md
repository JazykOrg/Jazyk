Criterion: A scene record contains four entries labeled point of view, setting, goal, and outcome.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present each scene as a record carrying four labeled entries: point of view, setting, goal, and outcome.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. Each scene in the manuscript is presented as a record (or a worked scene) that names a point of view, a setting, a goal, and an outcome.
3. The point of view entry names the character whose head the scene stays in.
4. The setting entry names one of the places in settings.
5. The goal entry states what the point-of-view character wants when the scene opens.
6. The outcome entry states what the point-of-view character has when the scene closes.

## Verdict contract

- pass: the manuscript contains scene records, each carrying all four labeled entries (point of view, setting, goal, outcome).
- fail: the manuscript lacks scene records, or any scene record is missing one of the four labeled entries.
