# crit_readme_24_e49d13ce

Criterion: For a scene with a point-of-view character, the narration stays inside that character's head throughout the scene.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep each scene's narration inside its point-of-view character's head:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. Each scene has a point-of-view character (the character whose head the scene stays in).
3. Throughout the scene, the narration stays inside that character's head: it reports only what that character perceives, thinks, or knows, and does not slip into another character's interiority.
4. No scene drifts into a different character's head mid-scene, and no scene is narrated from outside any character's head.

The requirement is a constraint: the point of view is the character whose head the scene stays in. A scene that narrates from another character's interiority, or from a headless omniscient vantage, violates the criterion.
