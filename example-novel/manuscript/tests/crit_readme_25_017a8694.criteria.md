Criterion: A scene's setting entry names a place that appears in the settings document.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present each scene with a setting entry, and that entry must name a place that appears in the settings document.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. Each scene in the manuscript carries a setting entry (a labeled field or an establishing passage that names where the scene takes place).
3. The place named by the setting entry appears in the settings document (settings.md or its equivalent): the same place, not a paraphrase or an invented location.
4. No scene's setting entry names a place that is absent from the settings document.

## Verdict contract

- pass: the manuscript contains scene content, and every scene's setting entry names a place that appears in the settings document.
- fail: the manuscript lacks scene content, or any scene's setting entry names a place that does not appear in the settings document.
