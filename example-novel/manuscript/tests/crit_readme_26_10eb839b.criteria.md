Statement hash: 84b96228d7017613

Criterion: At a scene's opening, the goal states what the point-of-view character wants; at the scene's close, the outcome states what that character has.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present each scene with a goal and an outcome, where the goal states what the point-of-view character wants when the scene opens and the outcome states what that character has when the scene closes.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. Each scene in the manuscript carries a goal that states what the point-of-view character wants at the scene's opening.
3. Each scene in the manuscript carries an outcome that states what that same point-of-view character has at the scene's close.
4. The goal and the outcome are tied to the scene's point-of-view character (the character whose head the scene stays in), not to another character.

## Verdict contract

- pass: the manuscript contains scene content, and every scene's goal states what its point-of-view character wants at the opening and its outcome states what that character has at the close.
- fail: the manuscript lacks scene content, or any scene's goal or outcome is absent, or any goal/outcome is tied to a character other than the scene's point-of-view character.
