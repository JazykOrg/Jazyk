# crit_readme_27_37037e40

Criterion: A scene names one point of view at its opening and does not switch to another point of view before the scene ends.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep each scene's narration inside a single point of view:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content organized into scenes, not only test or criteria files.
2. Each scene opens by establishing one point of view (one character's head, or a single consistent narrator).
3. No scene switches to a different point of view before the scene ends; the narration stays inside that one point of view for the whole scene.
4. No scene contains a mid-scene shift from one character's perspective to another character's perspective.

The requirement is a constraint: a scene never changes its point of view partway through. A scene that opens in one character's head and then shifts into another character's head before it ends violates the criterion.
