# crit_readme_4_576514b6

Criterion: A reader opening a chapter finds it presented as one or more scenes in sequence.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present every chapter as a run of one or more scenes in sequence:

1. Each chapter in the manuscript is composed of one or more scenes, not a single undifferentiated block of narration.
2. The scenes within a chapter appear in a sequential order (scene 1, then scene 2, etc.), not interleaved or out of order.
3. A reader opening any chapter can identify the scene boundaries and follow the sequence of scenes within that chapter.

## Verdict contract

- **pass**: Every chapter in the manuscript is structured as a sequence of one or more identifiable scenes.
- **fail**: At least one chapter is not presented as a run of scenes (e.g., it is a single undifferentiated block, or scenes are not in sequence).
