Criterion: In the premise, a reader sees Ines Marlow running the Lantern Room, a bookshop on the row of shops kept by her family for three generations.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript's premise must establish, in narrative form, that Ines Marlow runs the Lantern Room, a bookshop on the row of shops that her family has kept for three generations.

To pass:

1. The manuscript includes narrative content, not only test or criteria files.
2. The premise presents Ines Marlow as the one who runs the Lantern Room.
3. The Lantern Room is presented as a bookshop.
4. The bookshop is presented as standing on the row of shops.
5. The row of shops (and the bookshop with it) is presented as kept by Ines's family for three generations.
