Criterion: At Marlow House, a reader sees Ines living there alone, with no other resident shown in the house.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that Ines lives alone in Marlow House:

1. The manuscript identifies Marlow House as a dwelling.
2. The manuscript identifies Ines as a resident of Marlow House.
3. The text states or clearly implies that Ines lives there alone.
4. No other resident is shown living in Marlow House.
5. No surrounding passage contradicts Ines living alone in Marlow House.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if another resident is shown in the house, or if Ines's solitary residence in Marlow House is absent.
