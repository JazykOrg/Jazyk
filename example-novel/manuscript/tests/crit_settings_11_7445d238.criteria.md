Criterion: A reader locating the Lantern Room sees it presented as a bookshop on the quay in Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the Lantern Room as a bookshop on the quay in Port Alder:

1. The manuscript identifies the Lantern Room by name.
2. The manuscript identifies the Lantern Room as a bookshop (a shop that sells books).
3. The manuscript locates the Lantern Room on the quay.
4. The manuscript locates the quay in Port Alder.
5. No surrounding passage contradicts the Lantern Room being a bookshop on the quay in Port Alder.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the Lantern Room is not presented as a bookshop on the quay in Port Alder.
