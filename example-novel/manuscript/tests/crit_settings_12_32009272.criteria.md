# crit_settings_12_32009272

Requirement: req:settings-11
Statement hash: 9ac328888f45e97b

Criterion: When Ines needs money, the Lantern Room shows the shop on the ground floor and one room above it let to lodgers.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the Lantern Room's two-floor structure and its letting:

1. The manuscript identifies the Lantern Room.
2. The manuscript presents the Lantern Room as having two floors.
3. The manuscript places the shop on the ground floor.
4. The manuscript places a single room above the shop.
5. The manuscript states or clearly implies that Ines lets that upper room to lodgers when she needs the money.
6. No surrounding passage contradicts the two-floor structure or the letting of the upper room to lodgers.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript does not present the shop on the ground floor with a single room above it that Ines lets to lodgers when she needs the money.
