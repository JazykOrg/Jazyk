Criterion: On a day that is not Sunday, the Lantern Room is open at nine.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that the Lantern Room opens at nine on a day that is not Sunday:

1. The manuscript identifies the Lantern Room by name.
2. The manuscript states or shows that the Lantern Room opens at nine (the morning opening hour).
3. The opening at nine is presented for a day that is not Sunday (a weekday or Saturday), not a Sunday.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the Lantern Room opening at nine on a non-Sunday.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript does not establish that the Lantern Room is open at nine on a day that is not Sunday.
