Criterion: On Sunday, the Lantern Room is not open.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that the Lantern Room does not open on Sundays:

1. The manuscript identifies the Lantern Room by name.
2. The manuscript states or shows that the Lantern Room is closed on Sundays (it does not open on Sundays).
3. On a Sunday the Lantern Room is presented as not open.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the Lantern Room being closed on Sundays.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript does not establish that the Lantern Room is not open on Sundays.
