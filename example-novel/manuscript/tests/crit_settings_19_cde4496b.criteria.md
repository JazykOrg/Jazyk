Requirement: req:settings-1
Statement hash: 6a879122695e76d3

Criterion: A reader finds every book event located in Port Alder.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish that the whole book happens in Port Alder:

1. The manuscript identifies Port Alder as the setting (a harbor town on the west coast with about four thousand residents).
2. Every event, scene, and action in the manuscript is located in Port Alder or its immediate surroundings.
3. No event, scene, or action is located in a place outside Port Alder.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the claim that the whole book happens in Port Alder.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if any event is located outside Port Alder.
