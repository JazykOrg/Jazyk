# crit_settings_1_211b303c

Requirement: req:settings-9
Statement hash: 5a7c6de9dbb9adc8

Criterion: A reader of the Port Alder setting finds the harbor council owning the harbor wall and leasing the quay frontage to the row of shops standing on it.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the Port Alder harbor council's property and lease relationship:

1. The manuscript identifies the harbor council.
2. The manuscript identifies the harbor wall.
3. The text states or clearly implies that the harbor council owns the harbor wall.
4. The manuscript identifies the quay frontage.
5. The manuscript identifies the shops standing on the quay frontage, or an equivalent row of shops on that frontage.
6. The text states or clearly implies that the harbor council leases the quay frontage to those shops.
7. No surrounding passage contradicts the harbor council's ownership of the wall or lease of the quay frontage.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if either the ownership of the harbor wall or the lease of quay frontage to the shops is absent.
