# crit_settings_20_d17a2140

Requirement: req:settings-2
Statement hash: ab8e933b39f6bcdd

Criterion: A reader identifies exactly three places that matter in the book, with every other place serving only as a street between them.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish a setting in which exactly three places matter, and every other place is only a street the characters walk along between them:

1. The manuscript identifies exactly three places that matter (the three significant locations of the story).
2. The manuscript establishes that every other place is only a street the characters walk along between those three places.
3. No surrounding passage introduces a fourth place that matters, or treats a "street" place as itself significant.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript does not establish exactly three places that matter with every other place serving only as a street between them.
