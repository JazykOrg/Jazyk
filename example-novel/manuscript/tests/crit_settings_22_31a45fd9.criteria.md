# crit_settings_22_31a45fd9

Requirement: req:settings-19
Statement hash: 907230cd57f38550

Criterion: A reader comparing Marlow House with the Lantern Room lease finds the house has been in the Marlow family as long as the lease and that the two are spoken of together.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish, in its own narrative text, that:

1. Marlow House has been in the Marlow family for as long as the Lantern Room lease has been held by the family (the two durations are stated to be equal).
2. Marlow House and the Lantern Room lease are spoken of together in the manuscript — they are mentioned in the same passage or linked so that a reader reads them as a pair.
3. No surrounding passage contradicts the claim that the house and the lease have been in the family for the same length of time.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript does not tie the house's tenure to the lease's tenure or does not speak of the two together.
