Requirement: req:settings-3
Statement hash: c9c1b05d01ec2a71

Criterion: A reader of the setting notes sees Port Alder described as a west-coast harbor town with about four thousand residents.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish Port Alder's basic setting:

1. The manuscript identifies Port Alder as a place.
2. The text describes Port Alder as a harbor town (a town built around a harbor).
3. The text places Port Alder on the west coast.
4. The text gives Port Alder a population of about four thousand residents.
5. No surrounding passage contradicts the harbor-town, west-coast, or four-thousand-resident description.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if any of the harbor-town, west-coast, or four-thousand-resident elements is absent.
