# crit_settings_24_9977e3f2

Requirement: req:settings-4
Statement hash: 16ac7ba8ff36bf7c

Criterion: A reader of the Port Alder setting entry sees the town climb a hill above the stone harbor wall and the quay run along the wall's top.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish the physical layout of Port Alder:

1. The manuscript identifies Port Alder as a town.
2. The manuscript states or clearly implies that the town climbs a hill.
3. The manuscript identifies a stone harbor wall.
4. The text states or clearly implies that the town climbs a hill above the stone harbor wall.
5. The manuscript identifies the quay.
6. The text states or clearly implies that the quay runs along the top of the wall.
7. No surrounding passage contradicts the hill-above-wall layout or the quay running along the wall's top.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the hill-above-wall layout or the quay running along the wall's top is absent.
