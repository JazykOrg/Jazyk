# crit_settings_25_63d12912

Requirement: req:settings-8
Statement hash: ba94ab59df1f0e61

Criterion: A reader of the Port Alder setting entry sees the quay carry six shops and the Lantern Room placed third from the ferry office.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must place the quay's shops on the page:

1. The manuscript shows the quay carrying a row of six shops.
2. The manuscript places the Lantern Room as the third shop from the ferry office.
3. No passage gives the quay a different number of shops or places the Lantern Room at a different position from the ferry office.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the quay carrying six shops with the Lantern Room third from the ferry office.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript gives a different number of shops on the quay or places the Lantern Room at a different position from the ferry office.
