# crit_settings_26_065ffc01

Requirement: req:settings-23
Statement hash: 2527746e87c501e9

Criterion: A report produced at the end of chapter 2 is absent because the written survey report takes three days to produce and cannot exist before chapter 3.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must establish, in its own narrative text, that:

1. The written survey report takes three days to produce (the three-day production time is stated or clearly conveyed in the narrative).
2. Theo's report does not exist before chapter 3 — no report is present, completed, or in hand at the end of chapter 2 or earlier.
3. No surrounding passage contradicts the claim that the report cannot exist before chapter 3 (no scene shows the report completed or available before chapter 3).

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript shows the report existing or completed before chapter 3, or does not convey the three-day production time that makes the report absent before chapter 3.
