Criterion: A scene in which a surveyor produces the written survey report shows the report being written from the field notebook rather than from memory.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must show, in its own narrative text, a scene in which a surveyor (Theo) produces the written survey report, and that scene must:

1. Depict the report being written from the field notebook — the surveyor consults, copies, or transcribes from the notebook while producing the report.
2. Make clear that the report is not written from memory: the notebook is the source the report is drawn from, not the surveyor's recollection.
3. Not contradict the claim elsewhere in the manuscript (no passage shows the report being composed from memory or without the notebook).

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself contains such a scene. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript shows the report being written from memory, or shows no scene in which the surveyor produces the report from the notebook.
