# crit_settings_6_9d925c91

Requirement: req:settings-15
Statement hash: 313ec91a49a4bd74

Criterion: A scene that locates the harbor ledger places it in the ferry office safe rather than in the Lantern Room shop.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must place the harbor ledger in the ferry office safe, not in the Lantern Room:

1. The manuscript identifies the harbor ledger by name.
2. The manuscript locates the harbor ledger in the ferry office safe (the safe in the ferry office that Dot keeps it in).
3. No scene locates the harbor ledger in the Lantern Room shop; the harbor ledger is not described as living in the shop.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the harbor ledger being kept in the ferry office safe.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript locates the harbor ledger in the Lantern Room shop instead of the ferry office safe.
