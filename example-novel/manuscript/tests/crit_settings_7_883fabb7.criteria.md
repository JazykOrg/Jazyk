Criterion: A page that mentions both ledgers identifies the harbor ledger and the takings ledger as separate ledgers.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep the two ledgers distinct on the page:

1. The manuscript mentions the harbor ledger and the takings ledger.
2. Where both ledgers appear, the manuscript identifies them as two separate ledgers (the harbor ledger and the takings ledger), not as one ledger or as interchangeable.
3. No passage confuses the harbor ledger with the takings ledger or treats them as the same ledger.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the two ledgers being distinct.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript confuses the harbor ledger and the takings ledger on the page.
