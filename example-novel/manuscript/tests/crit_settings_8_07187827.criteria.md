Requirement: req:settings-7
Statement hash: 5e9aaca48e01e138

Criterion: A history reference for the harbor wall gives `1840` as the build year and `1988` as the last survey year.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must state the harbor wall's history on the page:

1. The manuscript gives `1840` as the year the harbor wall was built.
2. The manuscript gives `1988` as the year the harbor wall was last surveyed.
3. No passage assigns a different build year or a different last-survey year to the harbor wall.
4. No surrounding passage contradicts the wall having been built in 1840 and last surveyed in 1988.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself satisfies all pass conditions. Fail if the fact appears only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements, or if the manuscript gives a different build year or last-survey year for the harbor wall.
