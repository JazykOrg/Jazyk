# crit_style_10_2ce7432c

Criterion: A scene in an Ines chapter stays in Ines's point of view; a scene needing Theo's head appears in a Theo chapter instead.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep each chapter's point of view consistent across all its scenes:

1. Every scene that appears within an Ines chapter is narrated from Ines's point of view; the narration stays inside Ines's head.
2. No scene within an Ines chapter enters Theo's private thoughts, memories, perceptions, or close interior narration.
3. Any scene that requires Theo's head (Theo's interiority, his private thoughts or perceptions) is placed in a Theo chapter, not in an Ines chapter.
4. A reader can trace each scene in an Ines chapter to Ines's point of view, and each scene that needs Theo's head to a Theo chapter.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript:
- PASS when every scene in an Ines chapter stays in Ines's point of view, and every scene that needs Theo's head appears in a Theo chapter instead.
- FAIL when any scene in an Ines chapter enters Theo's head, or when a scene needing Theo's head is placed in an Ines chapter.
