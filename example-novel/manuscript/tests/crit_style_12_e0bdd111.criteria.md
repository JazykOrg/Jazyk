# crit_style_12_e0bdd111

Criterion: In chapter 3, the reader understands the lease clause from Ines reading it from the page, with no dialogue explaining the clause.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must convey the lease clause to the reader through Ines reading it from the page in chapter 3, and must not explain the clause in dialogue:

1. In chapter 3, the lease clause is presented to the reader by Ines reading it from the page (the text of the lease is shown as she reads it), so the reader understands the clause from that reading.
2. No character explains the lease clause in dialogue: the clause is not spelled out, paraphrased, or interpreted through spoken lines by any character.
3. The understanding the reader gains of the clause comes from the on-page reading in chapter 3, not from any character's verbal explanation.

## Verdict contract

This is a judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files in reading order and inspecting chapter 3 and the surrounding dialogue:

- PASS when, in chapter 3, the reader understands the lease clause from Ines reading it from the page, and no dialogue anywhere explains the clause.
- FAIL when the clause is explained in dialogue, or when the reader's understanding of the clause does not come from Ines reading it from the page in chapter 3.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
