# crit_style_13_f82029d6

Criterion: A reader finds the manuscript narration written in past tense.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must be narrated in the past tense:

1. The narration reports events as already having happened: verbs in the narrative voice are in the past tense (e.g. "she walked", "he said", "the ferry left").
2. The past tense is sustained across the whole manuscript, not just in isolated passages; a reader does not encounter present-tense narration ("she walks", "he says") as the narrative voice.
3. Dialogue may use present-tense speech naturally, but the surrounding narration that frames and reports it stays in the past tense.
4. No chapter shifts the narrative voice into the present tense.
