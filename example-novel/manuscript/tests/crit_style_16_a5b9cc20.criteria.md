# crit_style_16_a5b9cc20

Criterion: A chapter that breaks one manuscript style rule is marked not finished.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must treat its style rules as mandatory, not suggestions:

1. Every chapter that breaks at least one manuscript style rule (length, prose rules, tense and point of view, or any other rule stated in the style section) is marked not finished.
2. A chapter that breaks no style rule may be marked finished.
3. No chapter that breaks a style rule is marked finished.
4. The "not finished" marking is visible on the chapter itself (a status marker, a heading, or an equivalent signal a reader or editor can see), not merely implied.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files and the style rules:
- PASS when every chapter that breaks a style rule is marked not finished, and no chapter that breaks a rule is marked finished.
- FAIL when any chapter that breaks a style rule is marked finished or left unmarked, or when the "not finished" marking is absent or not visible.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
