# crit_style_19_274f6cd3

Criterion: Before chapter 8, no spoken dialogue by Ines or Theo contains the word "love".

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep the word "love" out of the two leads' spoken dialogue until chapter 8:

1. In every chapter before chapter 8 (chapters 1 through 7), no line of spoken dialogue spoken by Ines or by Theo contains the word "love".
2. The restriction applies to spoken dialogue only: narration, thought, or description that mentions "love" is not a violation; the word must not be said aloud by a lead.
3. The restriction applies to both leads: neither Ines nor Theo may say "love" aloud before chapter 8.
4. From chapter 8 onward the restriction no longer binds; a lead may say "love" aloud there.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files in reading order:
- PASS when, in every chapter before chapter 8, no spoken dialogue by Ines or Theo contains the word "love".
- FAIL when any spoken dialogue by Ines or Theo in a chapter before chapter 8 contains the word "love".

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
