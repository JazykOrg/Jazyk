# crit_style_1_16620b5c

Criterion: When Callum or Dot has an unstated thought, a reader sees that thought only through an action observed by Ines or Theo.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep Callum's and Dot's interiority externally observed:

1. Scenes involving Callum or Dot are narrated through Ines, Theo, or an equivalent perspective limited to what Ines or Theo can observe.
2. Any implied thought, feeling, intent, or private reaction belonging to Callum or Dot is conveyed through visible action, dialogue, expression, gesture, silence, or another observable behavior.
3. The manuscript does not directly enter Callum's or Dot's private thoughts, memories, perceptions, or close interior narration.
4. A reader can trace each implied Callum or Dot thought to something Ines or Theo sees them do.
