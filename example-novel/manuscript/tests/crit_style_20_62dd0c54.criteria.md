statementHash: 8e466aa8fa4155c2

Criterion: In each scene, the narration stays inside one character's head and reports only what that character sees, hears, and thinks.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must use close third-person point of view throughout:

1. Each scene is narrated from inside a single character's head; the narration does not jump between characters' interiority within a scene.
2. The narration reports only what that character sees, hears, and thinks; it does not report another character's private thoughts, feelings, or perceptions that the point-of-view character could not know.
3. No scene uses an omniscient or free-indirect narration that enters more than one character's interiority.
4. No scene is narrated in first person or in a detached third-person narration that reports interiority the point-of-view character cannot access.

## Verdict contract

Verdict pass when every scene in the manuscript satisfies conditions 1-4. Verdict fail when any scene violates any condition.
