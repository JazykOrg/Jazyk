# crit_style_21_26447605

Criterion: A scene contains no narrator comment from outside that scene.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript's narration must stay inside the scene and never step outside it to comment:

1. The narration reports only what the scene's point-of-view character sees, hears, and thinks; it does not break the scene to address the reader, judge the action, or summarize from a vantage outside the scene.
2. No passage of narration offers the narrator's own opinion, moral, or commentary on the events as they unfold (e.g. "how strange it was," "little did she know," "as fate would have it," or any equivalent authorial voice speaking from outside the scene).
3. The narration does not foreshadow, predict, or reveal outcomes the point-of-view character does not yet know, as a comment from outside the scene.
4. Every scene reads as if the narrator is inside the scene with the point-of-view character, not observing it from outside.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files:
- PASS when no scene contains narrator comment from outside the scene; the narration stays inside the point-of-view character's head throughout.
- FAIL when any scene contains authorial commentary, judgment, foreshadowing, or any voice speaking from outside the scene.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
