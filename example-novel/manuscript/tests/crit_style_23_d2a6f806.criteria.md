# crit_style_23_d2a6f806

Criterion: A scene other than the first scene of chapter 6 does not open with weather.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must obey the prose rule that no scene opens on the weather, with a single exception:

1. Every scene that is not the first scene of chapter 6 must not open on the weather. Its opening lines must not begin by describing weather (rain, storm, wind, sun, clouds, temperature, or any atmospheric condition) as the first thing the scene presents.
2. The single permitted exception is the first scene of chapter 6, which is allowed to open on the storm warning; this criterion does not apply to that scene.
3. A scene that opens on weather other than the first scene of chapter 6 violates the rule.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript scene files:
- PASS when no scene other than the first scene of chapter 6 opens on the weather.
- FAIL when any scene other than the first scene of chapter 6 opens on the weather.

When no scene files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
