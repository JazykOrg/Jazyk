# crit_style_24_248f2a83

Criterion: The first scene of chapter 6 opens on the storm warning.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must honor the single exception to the no-weather-opening rule:

1. The first scene of chapter 6 opens on the storm warning: its opening presents the storm warning as the first thing the scene shows.
2. The opening is the storm warning specifically, not generic weather; the scene begins by introducing the warning of the storm.
3. This is the one scene permitted to open on weather, and it must do so on the storm warning.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript scene files:
- PASS when the first scene of chapter 6 opens on the storm warning.
- FAIL when the first scene of chapter 6 does not open on the storm warning, or when chapter 6 has no first scene.

When no scene files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
