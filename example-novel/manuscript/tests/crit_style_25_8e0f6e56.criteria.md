# crit_style_25_8e0f6e56

Criterion: When a scene needs the other lead's head, that scene appears in the other lead's chapter.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must place every scene that requires the other lead's point of view inside that other lead's chapter:

1. A scene whose narration needs Ines's head (her thoughts, her perception, her interiority) appears in an Ines chapter, not in a Theo chapter.
2. A scene whose narration needs Theo's head appears in a Theo chapter, not in an Ines chapter.
3. No scene that needs the other lead's head is left in the chapter of the lead who is not that head.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files in reading order and inspecting, for each scene, whose head the narration requires and which chapter the scene sits in:
- PASS when every scene that needs a lead's head is placed in that lead's chapter.
- FAIL when any scene that needs the other lead's head is placed in the wrong lead's chapter.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
