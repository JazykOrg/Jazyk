# crit_style_26_742489a9

Criterion: Within a chapter, a scene that does not need the other lead's head keeps the chapter's point of view.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep every scene that does not need the other lead's head inside the chapter's own point of view:

1. Within an Ines chapter, a scene that does not need Theo's head is narrated from Ines's point of view.
2. Within a Theo chapter, a scene that does not need Ines's head is narrated from Theo's point of view.
3. No scene that does not need the other lead's head drifts into the other lead's head within its chapter.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files in reading order and inspecting, for each scene that does not need the other lead's head, whether its narration stays in the chapter's point of view:
- PASS when every such scene keeps the chapter's point of view.
- FAIL when any scene that does not need the other lead's head is narrated from the other lead's head within its chapter.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
