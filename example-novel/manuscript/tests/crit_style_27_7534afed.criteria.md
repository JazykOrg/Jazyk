# crit_style_27_7534afed

Criterion: A Theo measurement in the notebook is written in `metres` or `centimetres` and not in `feet`.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must give every measurement Theo writes in his field notebook in metric units:

1. Every measurement Theo records in the notebook is expressed in `metres` or `centimetres`.
2. No measurement Theo records in the notebook is expressed in `feet` (or any imperial unit).
3. The rule holds for every measurement Theo writes, on site and in the evening copy, not just in isolated passages.
4. The reason the units are metric is that this is how Theo writes them in the notebook.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files:
- PASS when every measurement Theo writes in the notebook is in `metres` or `centimetres` and none is in `feet`.
- FAIL when any measurement Theo writes in the notebook is given in `feet` or another imperial unit.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
