# crit_style_2_49fbd60d

Criterion: Across Callum and Dot scenes, a reader never receives narration from Callum's or Dot's point of view.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must keep Callum and Dot out of the point of view:

1. Callum appears only through narration, observation, dialogue, memory, or interpretation available from another allowed point-of-view character.
2. Dot appears only through narration, observation, dialogue, memory, or interpretation available from another allowed point-of-view character.
3. No scene or passage is narrated from Callum's close perspective.
4. No scene or passage is narrated from Dot's close perspective.
5. No passage gives direct private interiority, sensory filtering, or point-of-view authority to Callum or Dot.
