# crit_style_7_f8785a6d

Criterion: A chapter that comes in short is cut from the outline and the plot page is changed to match, with no padding added.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must never pad a short chapter:

1. No chapter is lengthened by adding filler, repetition, digression, or low-stakes material whose only purpose is to reach a target length.
2. When a chapter comes in short, the remedy is to cut it from the outline, not to expand it.
3. When a chapter is cut, the plot page is changed to match the cut, so the plot and the manuscript stay consistent.
4. A reader does not encounter padding: no passage exists that a reader would recognize as added only to fill space.

## Verdict contract

This is a negative, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript and the plot page:
- PASS when no chapter shows padding and any short chapter was cut from the outline with the plot page changed to match.
- FAIL when any chapter is padded to reach a length, or when a short chapter was padded instead of cut, or when a cut was not reflected on the plot page.
