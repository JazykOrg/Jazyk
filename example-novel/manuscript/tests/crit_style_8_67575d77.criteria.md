# crit_style_8_67575d77

Criterion: Chapter 1 belongs to Ines, and Chapter 2 belongs to Theo.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must assign point of view by chapter number, odd to Ines and even to Theo:

1. Chapter 1 is narrated from Ines's point of view: it opens in Ines's head (the source notes it opens in Ines's head watching the ferry come in).
2. Chapter 2 is narrated from Theo's point of view: it is told from Theo's perspective.
3. The pattern is consistent with the rule that odd-numbered chapters belong to Ines and even-numbered chapters belong to Theo.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the manuscript chapter files in reading order and inspecting the point of view of each chapter:
- PASS when Chapter 1 is narrated from Ines's point of view and Chapter 2 is narrated from Theo's point of view.
- FAIL when Chapter 1 is not Ines's point of view, or Chapter 2 is not Theo's point of view.

When no chapter files exist yet, the manuscript is not generated and the criterion fails by design: it is the acceptance gate generation must clear.
