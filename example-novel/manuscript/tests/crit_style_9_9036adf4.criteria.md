# crit_style_9_9036adf4

Criterion: A reader finds Chapter 1 opening in Ines's head while she watches the ferry come in and Chapter 8 closing in Theo's head.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must open and close on the two leads' heads as the point-of-view rule fixes:

1. Chapter 1 (manuscript/01-arrival.md) opens in Ines's head: its first scene is narrated from Ines's point of view, and the opening shows her watching the ferry come in.
2. The opening of Chapter 1 is Ines's interiority, not Theo's or an external camera: the reader is inside Ines's perception as the ferry arrives.
3. Chapter 8 (manuscript/08-the-lantern-room.md) closes in Theo's head: its final scene is narrated from Theo's point of view, and the manuscript ends inside Theo's perception.
4. The closing of Chapter 8 is Theo's interiority, not Ines's or an external camera: the last words the reader reads are Theo's.

## Verdict contract

This is a positive, judgment-based criterion. It is judged by reading the opening of Chapter 1 and the closing of Chapter 8:
- PASS when Chapter 1 opens in Ines's head watching the ferry come in and Chapter 8 closes in Theo's head.
- FAIL when Chapter 1 does not open in Ines's head watching the ferry, or when Chapter 8 does not close in Theo's head, or when either opening or closing is narrated from the wrong lead's head or from an external camera.
