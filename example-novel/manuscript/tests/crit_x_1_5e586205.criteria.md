Criterion: The harbor wall survey evidence consists of four items: field notebook, walkover, core samples, and report.

## Pass conditions

The manuscript must present the harbor wall survey as having exactly four evidence items:

1. The field notebook (Theo's notebook, carrying the survey measurements and evidence) is presented as survey evidence.
2. The walkover is presented as a part of the harbor wall survey.
3. The core samples are presented as a part of the harbor wall survey.
4. The report is presented as a part of the harbor wall survey.

All four items must appear together as the evidence of the harbor wall survey, with the field notebook included alongside the walkover, core samples, and report. No surrounding passage may contradict the field notebook being survey evidence.

## Verdict contract

Pass only if the completed manuscript itself presents the harbor wall survey with these four evidence items, the field notebook among them. Fail if the field notebook is absent from the survey evidence, or if the four items appear only in criteria files, planning notes, or source requirements rather than in the manuscript.
