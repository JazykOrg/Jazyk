# The report, written

The report was the third part of the survey, and it was the part that decided what happened to the wall, and Theo wrote it the way every surveyor wrote it, and the way was the thing that made the notebook carry the plot.

Theo did not write the report from memory. He sat at the table in the upstairs room with the field notebook open before him, the notebook he had kept between the walkover and the core samples, and he copied from it the way a copyist copies, line for line, the measurements he had written down by the wall and the readings he had taken from the cores. He did not recall them; he did not reach for them in his head. He set his pen to the report's page and he looked down at the notebook and he wrote what the notebook held, the measurement on the wall and the measurement again that evening, the two of them on the page, and he set them down in the report the way they stood in the notebook, and the report was the notebook set down on paper for the council, not the notebook recalled from his head.

That was the way a surveyor wrote the report, and the way was the thing that made the notebook carry the plot. The report was only as true as the notebook, and the notebook was the thing that could not be faked, the thing that held the crack whether or not the report did. Theo wrote the report from the field notebook rather than from memory, and because he wrote it from the notebook, the notebook was the source the report was drawn from, and the notebook was the thing that carried the plot, the thing that held the crack that the report, when it came, would not carry.

A written survey report took three days to produce, and the three days were three days of copying the notebook and setting the readings down for the council, and so the report could not exist before chapter 3. At the end of chapter 2 the report was not in hand, not finished, not on any table; it was the thing that would come three days later, and the three days were the reason the report was absent from the first half of the book.
