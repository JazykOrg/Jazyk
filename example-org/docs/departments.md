# Departments

Ridgeline Outfitters has four departments: Operations, Sales, Finance, and People. This
document lists what each one does, the roles inside it, and its teams.

## Employees and managers

Every employee belongs to exactly one department. Every employee has exactly one manager.
A manager is an employee with direct reports.

## Teams

Every department is organized into one or more teams. A team has a lead, a size, and a
list of members. The lead of a team is a manager. The size of a team is the number of its
members.

## Operations

Operations runs the Boulder warehouse and the four stores. Operations receives every
shipment from a supplier, counts it in, and moves it to the store that ordered it.

Operations has two roles:

- Store Manager: runs one store, schedules its staff, and owns its shrinkage number.
- Warehouse Associate: receives shipments, picks online orders, and counts stock.

## Sales

Sales owns the revenue target of each store and every wholesale account. Sales sets the
price list for wholesale accounts once per year.

Sales has two roles:

- Sales Associate: helps customers on the floor and rings up sales at the register.
- Account Manager: manages wholesale accounts and negotiates their pricing.

## Finance

Finance keeps the books, runs payroll, and reimburses expense claims. Finance closes the
books within 5 business days after the end of each month. Payroll runs on the 25th of
each month.

Finance has two roles:

- Accountant: keeps the ledger and closes the month.
- Payroll Specialist: runs payroll and answers pay questions.

## People

People recruits, onboards, and supports employees. People owns the hiring pipeline
described in [Hiring](hiring.md).

People has two roles:

- Recruiter: runs the hiring pipeline for every open role.
- People Partner: answers benefits and policy questions from employees.
