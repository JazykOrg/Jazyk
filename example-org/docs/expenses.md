# Expenses

An employee who spends their own money on company business files an expense claim to get
it back. Finance reimburses approved claims through payroll.

## The claim

An expense claim carries a total and a cost center. An expense claim carries one receipt
for every line. A claim covers purchases from a single calendar month.

Claims are filed on form EX-12 in the expense tool. Scanned receipts are stored under
`S:\Finance\Claims\<year>\<employee>`. Cost centers use the codes from the ledger, for
example `CC-4410` for the Boulder warehouse.

## Steps

1. The employee submits the expense claim to their manager with a receipt attached to
   every line.
2. The manager reviews the claim and either approves it or returns it to the employee.
3. The approvals a claim needs depend on its total:

   | Claim total          | Approvals needed                |
   | -------------------- | ------------------------------- |
   | 500 dollars or less  | the manager                     |
   | above 500 dollars    | the manager, then Finance       |

4. When the claim total is above 500 dollars, the manager forwards the approved claim to
   Finance for its approval.
5. Finance reimburses an approved claim in the next payroll run.
6. If a receipt is missing, the manager returns the claim to the employee, and the
   employee resubmits it with the receipt within 10 business days.
7. If Finance finds a line that is not a business expense, Finance rejects the claim and
   tells both the employee and the manager why.

## Timing

The manager reviews a claim within 5 business days of submission. Finance reviews a claim
it receives within 5 business days.

## Example

Dana, a sales associate, files a claim for 620 dollars after a trade show. Dana's manager
approves it on Tuesday, forwards it to Finance on the same day, and Finance approves it
on Thursday. The claim is paid in the payroll run on the 25th.
