# Hiring

People runs hiring. A hiring manager opens a requisition for a role on form HR-04, and
the recruiter runs the pipeline below for every application against that requisition.
Requisitions are filed under `People/Hiring/Requisitions/` on the shared drive.

## Who is involved

- The candidate is a person outside the company who applied for an open role.
- The recruiter is the People role that owns the pipeline.
- The hiring manager is the manager the open role reports to.
- The interview panel is two or three employees the hiring manager picks for the loop.

## Stages

An application is in exactly one stage at a time. The stages are:

- applied: the candidate submitted the application through the careers page at
  `ridgeline.example/careers`.
- screened: the recruiter has finished the phone screen.
- interviewed: the interview panel has finished the loop.
- offered: an offer letter is with the candidate.
- hired: the candidate is on the payroll.
- rejected: the application is closed and the candidate has been told.

## Moving between stages

- An applied application becomes screened when the recruiter completes the phone screen
  with the candidate.
- An applied application becomes rejected when the recruiter finds no match at the phone
  screen.
- A screened application becomes interviewed when the interview panel completes the loop
  with the candidate.
- A screened application becomes rejected when the interview panel declines to run a
  loop.
- An interviewed application becomes offered when the hiring manager decides to hire,
  provided both of the candidate's references check out.
- An interviewed application becomes rejected when the hiring manager decides not to
  hire.

The recruiter records every stage change with its date and the person who made it. The
recruiter tells the candidate about every stage change within 2 business days.

## Timing

The recruiter completes the phone screen within 5 business days of the application. The
hiring manager decides within 3 business days of the loop. An offer stays open for 7
calendar days.

## Background

Ridgeline hired its first recruiter in 2019, when the company passed fifty employees.
Before that, store managers hired their own staff and kept the paperwork in a binder at
the Pearl Street store. The pipeline in this document replaced the binder in 2021, and
the tracker replaced a spreadsheet the year after. No offer goes out before People has
approved the salary band for the role. The salary bands themselves are reviewed every
spring.
