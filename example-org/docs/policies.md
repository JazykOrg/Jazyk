# Policies

These policies apply to every employee. Each policy has a number on the intranet
(POL-101 and so on); the number is a filing label, not part of the rule.

## Time off (POL-101)

Every employee takes at least 10 days of paid time off per year. An employee requests
time off on form HR-21 at least 5 business days ahead. A manager answers a time-off
request within 2 business days.

## Remote work (POL-102)

An employee works remotely at most 2 days per week. A warehouse associate works on site
every day. A sales associate works on site every day.

## Span of control (POL-103)

A manager has at most 8 direct reports.

## Spending (POL-104)

An expense claim above 250 dollars needs the Finance department's approval in addition
to the manager's approval. The Finance department pays no claim without a receipt for
every line.

## Safety (POL-105)

Every employee completes the safety training within 30 days of their start date. Every
store and the warehouse runs a fire drill once per quarter.

## Customer service (POL-106)

Sales answers every customer email promptly. A sales associate greets every customer
who enters the store.

## Benefits (POL-107)

The People department answers a benefits question within 2 business days.

## Glossary

These terms appear in the handbook with their ordinary meaning:

- Business day: a weekday that is not a public holiday.
- Calendar day: any day, weekends and holidays included.
- Cost center: the accounting bucket a purchase is charged to.
- Per diem: a fixed daily allowance for meals while traveling, a term borrowed from
  accounting.
- Shrinkage: the retail term for stock lost to theft, damage, or miscounting.
- Wholesale: selling goods in bulk to another business rather than to a consumer.
