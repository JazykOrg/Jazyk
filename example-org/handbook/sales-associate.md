# Sales Associate

A Sales Associate is a Sales role that helps customers on the floor and rings up sales
at the register.

## On the floor

A Sales Associate helps customers on the floor and rings up sales at the register.

A sales associate greets every customer who enters the store.

## Work location

A sales associate works on site every day.

## Example

Dana, a sales associate, files an Expense Claim for 620 dollars after a trade show.
