# Sales

Sales is a Ridgeline Outfitters department that owns revenue and the wholesale accounts.

## Departments

Ridgeline Outfitters has four departments: Operations, Sales, Finance, and People.

## What Sales owns

Sales owns the revenue target of each store and every wholesale account. Sales sets the
price list for wholesale accounts once per year.

## Roles

Sales has two roles:

- Sales Associate: helps customers on the floor and rings up sales at the register.
- Account Manager: manages wholesale accounts and negotiates their pricing.

## Customer service

Sales answers every customer email promptly.
