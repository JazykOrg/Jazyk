# Ops

Ops is the practice that keeps the [Frontend](frontend.md) and the
[Backend](backend.md) running. Ops has four parts:

- [Monitoring](monitoring.md): watching the running services and paging when something
  is wrong.
- [Deployment](deployment.md): shipping a new version to production.
- [Backups](backups.md): keeping copies of the Database.
- [Support](support.md): answering the tickets Users open.

Each part keeps a runbook, and the engineering team reviews every runbook once per
quarter.
