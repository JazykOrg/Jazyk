# Support

Support is the part of [Ops](ops.md) that answers the tickets [Users](user.md) open.

## Tickets

- A User opens a ticket from the help menu of the [Frontend](frontend.md) with a
  subject and a description.
- Support answers every ticket within 1 business day.
- Support closes a ticket when the User confirms the answer or after 14 days without a
  reply.
- Support tags every ticket with the Organization it concerns and with one of the
  categories `billing`, `bug`, `question`, or `feature`.

## Escalation

- Support opens a bug report for the engineering team when a ticket describes a
  defect.
- Support refunds an [Invoice](billing.md#invoice) when the User was charged for a
  Plan the Organization did not use, and records the refund on the Invoice.
