What you are learning

Jazyk is a natural language compiler. It keeps a persistent semantic graph behind your documents: the things you name, their obligations, observable criteria, and the relationships and flows they imply.

A document can describe a program, a deck, an organization, or a story. Compilation gives you a model to inspect. Generation produces the artifact the model requires. Verification asks whether the artifact meets its criteria.

Documents → Graph → Product → Evidence

A path through the compiler

01

Get a workspace ready

Install locally or open Cloud. Choose your editor and AI.

→ 02

Structure the documents

Give each subject a home. Connect it with headings, links, and sets.

→ 03

Write the intent clearly

State obligations and concrete examples of success and failure.

→ 04

Read what Jazyk understood

Follow prose to entities, requirements, criteria, and diagrams.

→ 05

Turn the graph into a product

Bind existing work or generate an artifact, then check its evidence.

→

Then learn to change it

Once the first result is useful, the next question is how it evolves. Learn what an edit makes stale, how a decision becomes part of the docs, and how to start with something that already exists.

For command details and AI setup, use the CLI reference and models and benchmarks. They support the workflow rather than define it.
